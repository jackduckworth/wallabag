TV tonight: an Emmy-nominated US period drama finally lands on UK TV

====================================================================================================

FELLOW TRAVELERS

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
From the makers of Homeland, this suave, weighty, Emmy-nominated period drama finally lands on UK
telly. It is both a romance and a thriller, following Hawk (Matt Bomer) and Tim (Jonathan Bailey)
who meet in Washington DC during the height of McCarthyism, and develop a sexual relationship across
the next three decades. _Hollie Richardson_

THE REPAIR SHOP: BEYOND THE BARN

8PM, BBC ONE 
More emotional tales from inside the workshop and across the UK. Will a record player with a
connection to three continents be brought back to life? And is there any hope for a 1950s Rolleiflex
camera that once snapped portraits of the royals, but is now in need of a highly delicate
restoration? _Hannah J Davies_

PORTRAIT ARTIST OF THE YEAR 2026

8PM, SKY ARTS

[A man in a green jacket with grey hair and glasses and a man with curly brown hair wearing a dark
suit clasping his hands in front of his face look at a picture on an easel with a woman smiling
standing next to it]
Louis Theroux is the first brave celebrity to sit for his portrait in this week's competition. "If
you do splotches, that will be fine," he assures them. Former White House communications director
Anthony Scaramucci and actor Joanna Scanlan also take a seat. As is becoming tradition, there will
be tears for one of them. _HR_

THE BOOK THAT CHANGED MY LIFE

9PM, BBC TWO 
Queen Camilla inherited a love of reading from her father, who found comfort in literature while
held in a German prisoner-of-war camp. In this one-off documentary, she shares more about her
passion for books before meeting four people whose lives have also been changed by stories. _HR_

WINTER

9PM, ITV1
Overblown presentation chafes against a bog-standard whodunnit as Bristol cops look into the double
shooting of a couple in their own home, but Richard Armitage's brilliant eponymous pathologist
provides all the breakthroughs. Humdrum secrets and betrayals are treated as if they're intense
psychodramas._ Jack Seale_

GRAND DESIGNS

9PM, CHANNEL 4 
Twenty-five years ago, Chris and Gill took on a 19th-century woollen mill near Huddersfield,
restoring its historic shell while packing the inside with steel, glass and enough exorbitant
hi-tech gadgetry to rival a Bond villain. Now Kevin McCloud returns to say "Hmmm". Does the couple's
radical approach still look cutting-edge – or simply very, very 2001? _Ali Catterall_
