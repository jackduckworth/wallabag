One of Pop’s Hottest Stars Is Uniquely Refreshing. I Think I Know the Secret to Her Success.

====================================================================================================

Since Sunday, a video of Swedish pop star Zara Larsson performing at the Belgian music festival
Pukkelpop has been going viral—not because it’s controversial, but because of decidedly more
delightful reasons. In the clip, the 28-year-old Swede is performing her smash hit “Midnight
Sun”—you might recognize the tune from itsendless undulating chorus—but winds up bursting into
an ongoing fit of giggles when some fans in the audience begin simultaneously lifting each other up
into the air, with the airborne fans mimicking the song’s “dolphin” choreography. At first,
she’s shocked by the sheer number of fans suddenly being held up in the air, but when she calls
for more, the audience fulfills her request tenfold, until the crowd becomes a sea of bodies
hilariously holding other bodies aloft. This sets Larsson off into a fit of bubbling laughter, her
attempts to go back to singing thwarted by more chortling at her fans.

The joy in the video is infectious, which perhaps explains how the clip has gone so viral and earned
Larsson praise as a prime example of a pop star. It’s the cherry on top of a great year for the
artist, who has finally reached new heights in her career after years in the game. Larsson found
success at a young age in Scandinavia in the early 2010s but has struggled to become a household
name in the U.S., even as her earlier singles—“Lush Life,” “Ain’t My Fault,” “Never
Forget You,” and her feature on Clean Bandit’s chart-topper “Symphony”—were massive hits
internationally. It was social media that breathed new life into “Lush Life” and
“Symphony”—a song that soundtracked a 2024 “hopecore” TikTok trend, wherein people would
superimpose text of negativepersonal confessions over an idyllic backdrop image of a sunny oceanic
scene with a dolphin leaping into the air—priming Larsson for a new level of success when she
released her fifth studio album, _Midnight Sun_, in 2025. Advancing the neon aesthetics and bright
sounds used in those earlier tracks and online, _Midnight Sun_ cemented Larsson’s popularity
stateside and gave her an easily identifiable brand. Just try Googling her name and you’ll see the
vibrant look and feel she’s come to be associated with: The screen fills with bubbles as twin
dolphins leap across each other in front of an airbrushed pink-and-purple rainbow.

Larsson’s ascent has been, in other words, overwhelmingly _positive_. In an era in which everyone
seems to debate the merits of the Tate McRaes and Addison Raes of the current pop landscape, no one
seems to be batting an eye at Larsson’s meteoric rise or foaming at the mouth to tear her down
like they do with so many other starlets. What’s the secret to Larsson’s enviable position?
It’s simple: She went back to the basics.

It’s become common for newer pop acts to work like machines as they try to establish themselves,
releasing single after single at breakneck speeds. (Just look at Katseye, a group that seems to
follow the K-pop industry standard that allows almost no breaks as a unit, even if its members
clearly need them.) The conventional wisdom these days is that attention is fickle, so audiences
will quickly tire of a song. But Larsson, on the contrary, has taken a canny approach by milking
“Midnight Sun” for all it’s worth. The single was initially released in the summer of 2025,
but the songstress is still performing it today. Larsson even promoted her summer-themed album
through the winter, taking the full year to perform its tunes everywhere, at every possible venue
and in every conceivable scenario, on television, at awards shows, at festivals, and during her
“Midnight Sun” tour. This longevity, rather than making people sick of the song, has just made
her stick around longer in the cultural consciousness, with fans turning her dance breaks into
online dance challenges, her twerking into viral videos, her bedazzled neon makeup looks into beauty
trends, and her standout vocal moments into singing challenges. It’s a way better alternative to
our favorite artists disappearing shortly after album releases.

Larsson has also gone back to the classic move of releasing a remix album for _Midnight Sun_, titled
_Midnight Sun: Girls Trip_, with new features for every single song. Similar to when Beyoncé and
Lady Gaga teamed up for their phone-themed singles that featured each other, Larsson has also done
quid pro quo collaborations with artists PinkPantheress and Tyla, who both featured on _Girls Trip_
after Larsson featured on each of their singles. “Stateside,” the PinkPantheress remix with
Larsson, introduced new audiences to the Swedish singer, going viral on TikTok and peaking at the
top of the BillboardGlobal 200 chart.

There are other reasons why Larsson has felt like a breath of fresh air, from her unfiltered
internet presence to her loud support for abortion rights (a stance that, she said, lost her a
$3 million brand deal) and disdain for Immigration and Customs Enforcement and the Trump
administration’s hard-lining against immigrants. But above all else, in an era when even vocal
powerhouse Ariana Grande is facing criticism for leaning further than before into breathy vocals,
Larsson has never once ceded the sheer power of her voice. Her astounding vocal endurance and
agility are always on full display, as she never lipsyncs. Larsson’s mic is always on, which is
perhaps best evidenced by this latest viral video: Her pauses for bursts of laughter only underscore
how impressive, and _live_, her singing actually is.

None of this is particularly revolutionary but, put together, makes up a pop package that just
_feels_ good. By doing things the way our pop foremothers used to, Larsson has proved that some
tried-and-true methods to becoming a pop star are far from broken. Larsson spent years honing her
craft and working toward her breakout international hit, polishing her performance, finding her lane
in terms of aesthetics and sound—and when she perfected it all, she ran with it, full force, and
has kept it up for more than a year now. May the midnight sun never set on her.
