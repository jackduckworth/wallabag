TV tonight: moving film about the Dunblane massacre 30 years on

====================================================================================================

THE DUNBLANE TAPES

9PM, CHANNEL 4
March marks the 30th anniversary of the murders of 16 children and one teacher at Dunblane primary
school. This moving documentary explores the day and its aftermath, when parents campaigned for the
banning of handguns for private use in the UK. It's a positive coda, but the horror is inescapable
and many clearly remain traumatised. "Those children were given to my care," says deputy headteacher
Fiona Eadington, "and I didn't protect them." _Phil Harrison_

DRAGONS' DEN

8PM, BBC ONE
Beauty mogul Jenna Meek returns as a Dragon for an episode that begins with a coffin being carried
into the Den: an entrepreneur wants to lay traditional dating apps to rest. There follow pitches for
a cross between an awning and a tent, and a solution to the wastage caused when headphones break._
Jack Seale_

THIS FARMING LIFE

8PM, BBC TWO
As BBC Scotland's gentle rural documentary series returns, we meet Lucy and Adam, who need a £4m
mortgage to move their English operation north of the border. At the other end of the spectrum are
Emma and Rob, balancing extra jobs and money-making schemes to keep their Highlands farm afloat.
_Hannah J Davies_

MURDER CASE: THE HUNT FOR ARLENE FRASER'S KILLER

9PM, BBC TWO
This two-part documentary explores one of Scotland's strangest missing persons investigations,
hearing from Arlene Fraser's sister and officers on the case. Arlene disappeared in 1998. Since
then, police have probed her marriage, followed doomed leads, deployed unusual tactics and,
essentially, discovered nothing. _Phil Harrison_

HUNTING OUTBACK GOLD

9PM, U&YESTERDAY

[The hunters wear sun hats in front of a mountain]
On day four of the reality adventure series, Brendan encounters a spider so huge and hairy it would
send most people screaming back to civilisation. But not these guys. Brendan and friend Jeff are
pressing on with their quest to find Harold Lasseter's legendary lost gold. _Ellen E Jones_

MURDER ON A KNIFE'S EDGE: TRUE CRIME PRESENTS

9PM, ITV1
After breaking up with his girlfriend of 18 years, father of three Wayne Coventry met Cordelia
Farrell. A toxic relationship ensued and in 2019 Farrell killed her partner. It's the first case in
the second series of the true-crime show, with Farrell's brother and father contributing and experts
examining the details. _HR_
