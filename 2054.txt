Later yesterday in the Oval Office, Trump had another _real big_ tantrum, at reporter Andrew
Feinberg, who handled it quite well:

Transcript:

> FEINBERG: You’re offering 10 percent for one percent for whistleblowers for turning in what
> you’re describing as fraud. [_Somebody corrects him and says “one percent.”_] One percent?
> That’s actually worse. The False Claims Act, which has been on the books since 1863,
> whistleblowers can get between 10 and 25 percent. Why would someone who has evidence of genuine
> fraud go to …
> 
> TRUMP: Because it’s a lot of money. And because when they did that, that’s a certain type of
> crime. But nobody ever thought that you could have stolen $2 trillion or whatever the ultimate
> number is going to be. [_Trump always talks about “two trillion dollars,” it’s just a number
> he makes up when he’s talking about finding fraud, or investments coming into the US. It’s
> just part of the imaginary wonderland he sees when he looks at the world through those squinty
> dementia peepers of his_. — Ed.] And at one percent, people will make millions and millions of
> dollars for turning in what actually is a somewhat routine crime of, you know, of millions of
> millions. But we’re talking about we’re talking about billions and actually probably trillions
> of dollars. [_Billions and trillions!_ — Ed.]

JD Vance chimed in at this point (not in the videos above, because nobody wants to look at that
chubby bunny face not even a mother could love). He whined that Feinberg’s question was
“fundamentally dishonest because over 99 percent of the fraud that we’re finding in the federal
government isn’t covered by the False Claims Act.” Yeah, we’re so sure. You know who just
_hates_ fraud? The Trump regime.

Grandpa’s tantrum came immediately after:

> TRUMP: Listen to you. Hey! I don’t know who the hell you are but _you better be careful_.
> 
> FEINBERG: Andrew Feinberg, I’m with the _Independent_ and …
> 
> TRUMP: Quiet! Quiet! What’s wrong with you? Are you a sick person?
> 
> FEINBERG: Not that I know of, sir.
> 
> TRUMP: I actually think there’s something wrong with you.

LOL what? Fucking brain-addled syphilis clown.

Feinberg tweeted after Gramps’s meltdown to clarify that “I had told him exactly who I was not
five minutes before this exchange.” He also wrote about what happened for _The Independent_.

The White House attempted to make this a totally normal and awesome Trump evisceration of a fake
news journalist, something for his piggy MAGA masses to eat. Vance dutifully tweeted for his
pathetic Daddy:

[President Donald J. Trump eviscerates imbecile “journalist” Andrew Feinberg for misbehaving in
the Oval Office: "What's wrong with you? Are you a sick person? I actually think there's something
wrong with you."] 
And he didn’t even say thank you! He didn’t even say thank you!

And of course, this, from the White House press account, complaining about what “Mongoloid
Moron” Andrew Feinberg had done, in case you forgot they were racist _and_ disgusting toward
disabled folks:

[. @POTUS absolutely roasts Mongoloid Moron @AndrewFeinberg for his disgraceful behavior: "What's
wrong with you? Are you a sick person? I actually think there's something wrong with you."] 
What had Feinberg done again? Oh yes, asked a question that made the Trump regime look fucking
stupid, like they do every single day. Also Trump couldn’t remember who he was, because he
hadn’t identified himself in five whole minutes.

>

His ass … shit, it’s like — it’s like a pile of muscle!
