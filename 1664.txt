TV tonight: Katie Price on her wild wedding and life with Peter Andre

====================================================================================================

KATIE PRICE: NOTHING TO HIDE

9PM, SKY DOCUMENTARIES
Twenty years before Taylor and Travis's stadium wedding bonanza, there was Katie Price and Peter
Andre's fairytale ceremony – or, as Kerry Katona puts it, "like Cinderella on coke and speed on a
seven-day bender". The third episode of Price's documentary focuses on the marriage she says
provided the best years of her life … until it didn't. "I'd love you to interview Pete," she says.
"The chances are zero." And she's right. _Hollie Richardson_

OUR LIVES: HEAVY HORSES

7pm, BBC One
Another little delight in the series that tells the stories of people around the UK. This time, Kate
Mobbs-Morgan in south Wales pulls at the heartstrings as she explains how she set up a workhorse
business after a cancer diagnosis in her 30s and why she particularly loves her first animal, Kipp:
"I couldn't love somebody more than him." _HR_

DEAL OR NO DEAL: BANKER'S BEST BATTLES

7.30PM, ITV1
This clip selection looks back on the more memorable moments from the hardy gameshow. This time,
Georgia and Matt do battle against the Banker – but will they take his offer or hold out in the
hope of a bigger reward? Stephen Mulhern is at the helm. _Phil Harrison_

CASUALTY 24/7: EVERY SECOND COUNTS

8PM, CHANNEL 5
Here, we spend 24 hours in the real life of Barnsley's A&E department. Sister Benita is attempting
to juggle rising patient numbers with a limited number of beds. And Jake, a plumber, has
mysteriously injured his ankle while walking his dog and keeps finding doggy treats secreted on his
person. _Priya Elan_

MY FLOATING HOME

9PM, MORE4

[Canal boats moored up.]

Peter and Tracy live on a narrowboat with a four-poster bed – but after 10 years, it's time for
something different. They take their big design dreams – including a modern kitchen – to boat
builder Chris Williams, who has 30 years' experience. But how will he fare with a £180,000 budget
and a three-month schedule? _HR_

PLAY FOR TODAY: EDNA, THE INEBRIATE WOMAN

10PM, BBC FOUR
A selection of landmark Play for Today episodes are available on iPlayer, including this 1971 one
written by Cathy Come Home's Jeremy Sandford. It follows the travails of homeless and jobless
elderly alcoholic Edna, played by Patricia Hayes, who won a Bafta for her performance. _HR_

FILM CHOICE

[A woman holds a bloody handkerchief as a man leans against a wall, his nose bleeding]
THE SECOND ACT (QUENTIN DUPIEUX, 2024), 12.45am, Film4
This surreal provocation from Quentin Dupieux could be about the self-importance of actors or the
dangers posed to human creativity by AI – but it might simply be a cheeky exercise in dramatic
rug-pulling. A group of performers (including Léa Seydoux and Vincent Lindon) play scenes – for
an AI director – that almost immediately fall apart as they go off-script, get into arguments,
fight, even quit. A meta comedy where you're never quite sure which film within a film you're
watching. _Simon Wardell_
