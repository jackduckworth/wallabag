TV tonight: Ellie White and Natasia Demetriou's side-splitting comedy

====================================================================================================

ELLIE & NATASIA

10PM, BBC TWO 
There is not one weak sketch in the opener of Ellie White and Natasia Demetriou's comedy – they
will have you weeping tears of laughter. Now in its second series, expect returning characters and
new ones. There are lifestyle influencers shopping at Asda; hipsters spending all day in an
overpriced trinkets boutique; and Mary Poppins at a gynaecological appointment. It also guest stars
Steve Coogan, Sian Clifford and Jamie Demetriou. _Hollie Richardson_

THE REPAIR SHOP: BEYOND THE BARN

8PM, BBC ONE 
This minor variation on the Repair Shop formula continues: the barn is still open but the gang
embark on a few road trips. Most notable this time is the Ukrainian tsymbaly (a hammered dulcimer)
belonging to a woman from Cornwall. Once it's been lovingly restored, it's moving to see it being
played. _Phil Harrison_

PORTRAIT ARTIST OF THE YEAR 2026

8PM, SKY ARTS 
The portrait competition where tears are expected by at least one of the celebrity sitters each week
returns. This time, they are Olympian skeleton skater Matt Weston, poet Nikita Gill, and film
director and actor Dexter Fletcher. _HR_

ASTEROIDS: SPARK OF LIFE

8PM, BBC FOUR

[A volcano blowing up]
Denis Lawson delivers a mighty science lesson with this programme, which tests a theory about
asteroids sparking life on Earth – rather than just extinguishing it. He rewinds 4bn years to tell
the story of the newly formed planet, as scientists share their mindblowing research. _HR_

9PM, CHANNEL 4 
In 2017, energy conservationist Andrew bet it all on Britain's first self-heating home, which would
harness and store the summer's warmth in vast insulated earth banks, to be deployed in winter. He'd
soon come up against structural problems, spiralling costs and a teed-off wife. Years later, Kevin
McCloud checks in to discover whether the project paid off. _Ali Catterall_

THE EXPERIMENT

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
The makers of Channel 4's next provocative social experiment have remained very hush hush about the
details. Mat Fraser and Sophie Morgan are the hosts at a residential facility where visitors are
invited to meet guests and "nothing is quite as it seems". _HR_

FILM CHOICE

MOANA (THOMAS KAIL, 2026) , DISNEY+

[Dwayne]
This must be the role Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson was born to play: muscular demigod Maui in the
live-action remake of Moana. The animated musical was an instant hit when it was first released in
2016, with moving, catchy songs written by the master Lin-Manuel Miranda, and a screenplay drawing
from Polynesian mythology. This version survives the transition to photorealism, thanks to Johnson's
winning brio and a star-making turn from Catherine Laga'aia as the ocean-faring lead. _Ellen E
Jones_
