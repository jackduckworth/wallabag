J.D. Vance Has Found a Remarkable New Way to Seem Like an Entitled Doofus

====================================================================================================

delivered to your inbox daily._

Wasn’t it just days ago that news outlets reported that Vice President J.D. Vance had the military
raise an Ohio river’s water level to make conditions more optimal for his 41st-birthday kayaking
trip? So how is it that he’s already moved on to wreaking havoc on an entirely different locale
for a whole separate getaway, this time to England’s Cotswolds, where, a few days ago, his
security team allegedly dispatched the police en masse in advance of his arrival? We are 12 days
into August, and the Vance travel monster has managed to upend life on two separate continents.

According to a dispatch from U.K.’s Observer, this was the scene:

> “We have had the police knocking on every door,” said a dog walker and resident of Dean, as a
> helicopter buzzed overhead. “They wanted the names of everybody living there and details of
> their social media. I know several people refused. We asked them if they were protecting us, or
> Vance. At least they were honest and said it is for him and that it will all be passed on to the
> American security people.”

The locals apparently found the authorities demanding to know if they’ve posted photos of Vance in
the style of a Furby to be “unsettling.” No kidding. (Kamala Harris reportedly visited the area
recently as well, but the most noteworthy thing about her stay was that she was spotted at a famous
pub.)

A pattern is beginning to emerge here. In his travels this year, the vice president has been known
to have places like the Colosseum, as well as the Taj Mahal and Disneyland, closed off to the public
while he and his family enjoy a private visit—behavior that may be typical for a foreign leader of
his notoriety but will do little to help his officious public image. Nor will raising the water
level of a river, something I’m pretty sure I didn’t even know was possible to do until I read
about his ordering the Army Corps of Engineers to work their magic on the Little Miami River.
Demands like this make him sound like some kind of mad king, no matter what excuses his camp makes
about safety concerns. (And, hey, the New York Times noted, Al Gore did this once too!)

Anyway, does Vance _really_ go on all that many vacations? The full rundown of Vance’s other
destinations this year—remember, he’s been in office only since January—includes Italy, India,
Vermont, Greenland, Disneyland, and Nantucket. Many of those were “official trips,” which
politicians from both parties take frequently, reasonably or not. And it’s not unusual for anyone
to spend time with their families in August. But as with many things in his life, Vance does seem to
have a unique knack for looking like a complete jerk when it’s his turn. The former Ohio senator
is already known for many dubious things—he is the guy who mocked “childless cat ladies,” the
guy who’s utterly convinced that he has a great sense of humor, and the guy who always looks as if
he has eyeliner on, to name a few—but being a guy who has main-character energy in foreign resort
towns is some kind of humiliating new low. It’s almost as bad as being a president who’s always
golfing.

On his trip to the Cotswolds, Vance is reportedly staying in an $11,000-a-week house, though the
cost of the trip to taxpayers for those social-media close reads will surely dwarf that by some
multiple. Despite all the headlines, I don’t think we have any real hope of making Vance feel
shame or humility. But on a purely surface level, next time he books a trip—the Daily Beast says
he has his heart set on getting to Hawaii one of these days—maybe he’ll at least think about how
this all looks to everybody else.
