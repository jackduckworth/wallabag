TV tonight: Martin Compston's guide to Las Vegas, his bizarre second home

====================================================================================================

MARTIN COMPSTON: LIVING LAS VEGAS

10PM, CHANNEL 4
Actor Compston is married to a woman from Vegas; this three-part documentary sees him trying to get
a handle on a place that has never quite felt like home. He's a cheerful guide to this bizarre city
of hype, neon and casinos, checking out a hotel room with its own basketball court and blowing a
hypothetical $20k on four hands of blackjack. Eventually, you understand both the excitement and the
reservations engendered by the place. _Phil Harrison_

THE SECRETS OF YOUR PHONE: THEY DON'T WANT YOU TO KNOW

7PM, CHANNEL 5
Are smartphones snooping on us? Because increasingly, it feels as if we're all buckling under an
avalanche of personalised ads. The truth may be more disturbing: for all the hoo-ha, we're still
supremely relaxed about handing over our "digital profiles". But we can, at least, erect some
guardrails, as Alexis Conran discovers. _Ali Catterall_

SHAKESPEARE & HATHAWAY: PRIVATE INVESTIGATORS

8PM, U&ALIBI
Love looks not with the eyes but with the mind. Will that help Stratford private eye duo Lu (Jo
Joyner) and Frank (Mark Benton) when a heartsick client hires them to infiltrate a sensory-based
matchmaking service? Judging by this opener, series six of the cosy crime drama is as fun as ever.
_Graeme Virtue_

20 YEARS OF NOT GOING OUT

9PM, BBC ONE

[Lee Mack leans up against a stack of three televisions showing episodes of the sitcom Not Going
Out]
Lee Mack's domestic sitcom is turning 20 – a feat that seemed unlikely when it was cancelled in
2009. It was given a stay of execution thanks to audience pressure and has thrived ever since:
according to this breezy documentary, the secret is its clever balance between modernity and
tradition. Season 15 begins immediately after. _PH_

9PM, CHANNEL 4
Emily and Peter have a bold rebuild plan that will replace their London home with a pair of
three-storey "cloud houses" inspired by the pastel colours and art deco curves of 1930s Miami
architecture. But will they pass muster as McCloud houses? Kevin monitors a typically tumultuous
project. _GV_

SAM SMITH: LIVE AT THE LONDON COLISEUM

10.40PM, BBC ONE
The Grammy/Oscar/Brit winner is on a stripped-back "intimate" tour with a new album, Hazel Eyes,
where their gorgeous vocals are front and centre. This specially recorded night at the London
Coliseum also closes with their greatest hit, Stay With Me. _Hollie Richardson_

FILM CHOICE

TOY STORY 5 (MCKENNA HARRIS AND ANDREW STANTON, 2026), DISNEY+

[Animated image of Bullseye the horse and Jessie the cowgirl, both look concerned]
Not particularly hot off the production line – Toy Story 4 was seven years ago – Woody, Buzz and
their boxful of plastic friends are back, but WALL-E director Andrew Stanton maintains the
franchise's beguiling focus on the joys and pitfalls of childhood. With the retired Woody (Tom
Hanks) absent, Joan Cusack's Jessie is the new sheriff in town/bedroom. But she faces an existential
threat when her owner, Bonnie, ostracised by her tech-savvy peers, gets a tablet – the superior
Lilypad "Lily" (Greta Lee) – and loses all interest in play. While having a dig at society's
digital obsessions, it's also a nostalgic eulogy to a world where pure imagination and the capacity
to be silly are cherished. _Simon Wardell_

THE LOVE HYPOTHESIS (CLAIRE SCANLON, 2026), PRIME VIDEO
Weirdly, the bestselling novel on which Claire Scanlon's campus romance is based started life as
Star Wars fan fiction about Rey and Kylo Ren. Not that you'd know it, save for the involvement in
this film of Tom Bateman, Daisy Ridley's husband, who stars as Adam, AKA "Dr Evil", a loathed
Stanford biology lecturer. He is persuaded by doctoral student Olive (Lili Reinhart) to pose as her
boyfriend, so her best pal, Anh (Rachel Marsh), will date a boy Anh thinks Olive still fancies. In
return, he will look more committed to the university and have his funding renewed. Love among the
lab coats ensues. _SW_
