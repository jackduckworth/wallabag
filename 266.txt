Eventually, my parasympathetic nervous system will activate and I will be capable of reason again,
but in this moment I can honestly say it feels as if Taylor Swift’s engagement, which the pop star
announced on Instagram on Tuesday, is the most important thing that has ever happened. That I know
I’m probably far from the only person who is having an outsize reaction to this news is a
testament to just how massively successful Swift has been over the course of her career in turning
her life, especially her romantic life, into a narrative in which millions of people are deeply
invested.

Swift and her fiancé, football player Travis Kelce, have been dating for two years, often in
extremely high-profile fashion, such as when their relationship became one of the main storylines of
last year’s Super Bowl. It’s an international obsession, and has been since Swift first showed
up at one of Kelce’s games in fall 2023. Despite the constant attention on the two of them as a
couple, news of the engagement comes as a bit of a shock: It was almost two weeks ago that Swift
went on Kelce’s podcast, _New Heights_, and the public was given its first real glimpse of what
the two are like together. Swift was there to announce her new album, _The Life of a Showgirl_,
which comes out in October, and one might have assumed that she would spend the weeks leading up to
its release drumming up interest by doing things like dropping a single and/or getting photographed
on the way to fancy dinners. Instead, surprise! She’s engaged.

Swift, now 35, has been in the public eye since she was a teenager, and collectively, we’ve
watched her go through a lot. Of course it’s emotional to see her engaged; many of us remember
when she was a young girl singing about the fantasy of getting engaged. That said, it was unclear if
a more traditional path involving things like white dresses was still in the cards for her—dreams
evolve, especially when you’re a pop star. Swift dated her previous boyfriend, Joe Alwyn, for six
years, and it was fair to wonder why they didn’t choose to get married. Did he not want to? Did
she? This engagement and the massive rock that accompanies it suggest to me that Swift might still
want some of those traditional things—marriage, maybe a family—and it’s going to be
fascinating to watch her navigate them as the most famous woman in the world.

When Tom Holland and Zendaya got engaged, they confirmed nothing, and we’ll likely never see
photos of their wedding. Swift, in contrast, knew that she needed to give her fans something to
feast on, and she delivered. There are so many questions I have about this that will likely never be
answered, but as a longtime observer, I—and millions of TikTokkers who instantly turned on their
ring lights upon seeing this news—can certainly hazard a few guesses.

Did Kelce plan the garden proposal, complete with photographer, himself? I feel like there’s no
way he did. (Their announcement came with the caption “Your English teacher and your gym teacher
are getting married 裡,” and I definitely think “gym teacher” is an accurate way of
describing something essential about Kelce.)

Swift is so meticulous, so in control—how would one even begin to go about getting her permission
to propose to her? Oh, God, he probably asked her dad for her hand.

And when did this happen, by the way? Was it masterminded by Swift’s team to time perfectly with
her album release, or was that just a happy coincidence? With a new album and football season on the
couple’s calendars, the timing feels too good to be dumb luck. I’m pretty sure Taylor Swift
doesn’t believe in dumb luck.

They’ll be signing history’s greatest prenup, right? Seems like you don’t go through
Scootergate and do anything less.

I can already imagine the spectacle the wedding is going to entail. In 2023, during the first few
months of the “Eras” tour, I remember thinking that Taylor Swift could not possibly get any more
famous, that she could not possibly keep this hot streak going, and every time, she found a way to
keep winning, to get even bigger, to occupy more space in my brain. And now she’s done it again.
