TV tonight: Dawn French is a hoot in insurance-scam comedy

====================================================================================================

CAN YOU KEEP A SECRET?

9.30pm, BBC One
Dawn French continues to be a hoot as Debbie, a woman who's pretending her husband has died for the
insurance payout. But now the cat's out of the bag: her police officer daughter-in-law Neha (Mandip
Gill) knows that William (Mark Heap) is alive and demands they give the money back. Meanwhile,
somebody else has worked out the scam and has used a very uninspiring font in a blackmail letter.
But Debbie has zero intention of cowering to any threats. Great fun. _Hollie Richardson_

LANDSCAPE ARTIST OF THE YEAR 2026

8pm, Sky Arts
St James's Park offers another octet of artists the chance to paint water, trees and, in the
distance, about a fifth of Buckingham Palace. The more unusual approaches include a surreal
interpretation based on AI, a vibes-based painting by a psychologist and a morass of greyish brown.
_Jack Seale_

[The artists reach St James' Park.]

DIGGING FOR BRITAIN

9pm, BBC Two
To the south of Britain, where archaeological sites include an Anglo-Saxon cemetery in Kent, an
18th-century slipway on the New Forest coast and underneath Trinity College, Oxford. There are also
iron age objects unearthed in Herne Bay, which are so delicate they are block-lifted out and X-rayed
– but what hides inside? _HR_

GRANTCHESTER

9pm, ITV1
Is witchcraft afoot in Robson Green's cosy crime drama? This is the question that Alphy and Geordie
wrestle with as they visit a foundling home. Although Geordie finds himself struggling with
something more troubling: the possibility of his partner's guilt. _Alexi Duggins_

PATIENCE

9pm, Channel 4
"Rock star archaeologist" Samuel Henry stands poised to declare a shocking revelation about a Viking
runestone when he suddenly drops dead in the middle of a symposium. As Patience and Frankie
investigate, the same fate befalls his colleague. Meanwhile, Patience tells Elliot what she needs
from a relationship, in the form of a densely printed contract. _Ali Catterall_

SURGEONS: A MATTER OF LIFE OR DEATH

9pm, Channel 5
Kidneys that have become the size of watermelons and a rare tumour growing at speed in a nose and
cheek are operated on in this week's nerve-racking episode of the fly-on-the-wall hospital series.
Can the surgeons remove them? _HR_

FILM CHOICE

[Priya Kansara and Ritu Arya in Polite Society.]
POLITE SOCIETY (NIDA MANZOOR, 2023), 9pm, Film4
The creator of We Are Lady Parts, Nida Manzoor, brings that show's vivid punk energy to her feature
film debut, a punchy comic thriller. Priya Kansara is Ria, a teenage British Muslim martial arts
fanatic who dreams of becoming a stunt performer. Her older sister, Lena (Ritu Arya), is an art
school dropout who somehow attracts the attention of wealthy, eligible geneticist Salim (Akshay
Khanna). Aghast at Lena abandoning her artistic dreams for an arranged marriage, Ria plots sabotage.
Can she find anything wrong with Salim – or is this just a bad case of sibling jealousy? _Simon
Wardell_
