TV tonight: inside the UK's response to alleged Israeli war crimes in Gaza

====================================================================================================

DISPATCHES: BRITAIN'S GAZA SECRETS EXPOSED

11.05PM, CHANNEL 4
With Donald Trump cheerleading for a far-right Israeli government, the horror in Gaza has
represented an extreme moral and political hazard for successive UK governments since the Hamas
attacks of October 2023. However, they've played a difficult hand pretty badly. This film gains
access to internal Whitehall documents to offer an insight into Britain's compromised response to
alleged Israeli war crimes. _Phil Harrison_

LUDWIG

9PM, BBC ONE
A corduroy-clad cosy crime climax of sorts as the second season of this slight, charming drama
ambles to a close. John and Lucy are faced with a murder that affects them personally. Will it
enable them to finally snare Joanne Kemper? David Mitchell and Anna Maxwell Martin continue to keep
us onboard for the low-intensity ride. _PH_

TASKMASTER

9PM, CHANNEL 4

[Smilling Nina Conti and Isy Suttie at a white tabletop with white plastic sheeting hanging behind
them]
Hippos, steam and googly eyes all feature in tonight's tasks, with Richard Ayoade, Isy Suttie and
Chloe Petts taking up the challenge. But the big question is: what will they find when they look
under tables? Meanwhile, Greg Davies shouts at art. Just your average Taskmaster episode, then.
_Hannah Verdier_

ALL CREATURES GREAT AND SMALL

9PM, CHANNEL 5
Veterinary heroism and incremental social change in post-second world war Yorkshire remain a
pleasing weekly tonic: this episode has the classic premise of thrusting ideologue Siegfried (Samuel
West) perturbing the locals by trying new things. If that weren't enough, Helen (Rachel Shenton)
experiments with a different type of cow: friesians! _Jack Seale_

RUPAUL'S DRAG RACE UK

1OPM, BBC THREE
This week, the category is musicals. Enter a jazz-hands-heavy take on Chicago. With the West End
theme locked and loaded, the guest judges are Mazz Murray (who trod the boards playing Dusty
Springfield) and Layton Williams. But which queen will take their final bow? _Priya Elan_

THE PAPER

11PM, SKY ONE
More of the US Office spin-off that sends up the embattled state of local journalism. This
double-header sees editor Ned (Domhnall Gleeson) endure a brush with the manosphere. Elsewhere, the
Truth Teller celebrates a big anniversary and CEO Marv (Allan Havey) tries to kill a story. _Hannah
J Davies_
