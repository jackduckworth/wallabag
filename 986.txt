TV tonight: a fun whodunnit drama in the 60s fashion world

====================================================================================================

GRANTCHESTER

9PM, ITV1
"One spread in Woman's Own and he thinks he's David Bailey …" The suspicious death of a
self-regarding fashion photographer threatens to derail the grand opening of Cathy Keating and Mrs
Chapman's new boutique in sleepy Grantchester. Thankfully, Rev Alphy and Cathy's copper husband
Geordie are on hand to tackle the knotty case, even if DI Keating seems equally determined to dig
further into his holy friend's family history. Might this cause some friction for the 1960s
crimefighting duo? _Graeme Virtue_

DRAGONS' DEN

8PM, BBC ONE
Sisters Olivia and Tanyka are giving up their jobs to spend more time on their money-saving platform
for new and expecting parents, Cubbi. They're hoping guest judge Susie Ma – beauty empire founder,
former Apprentice star and new mum – will be keen. But there's another Dragon who sees the birth
of something potentially great. _Hollie Richardson_

GEORGE CLARKE'S BUILDING HOME

8PM, CHANNEL 4
If Grand Designs is too much of a white-knuckle ride, this series from the Amazing Spaces host is
considerably gentler. Not that the builds are without their challenges: as Tina and Steve rip down
their Anglesey farmhouse to start from scratch, even an £800,000 budget proves tight._ Hannah J
Davies_

THE APPRENTICE

9PM, BBC ONE
It's hard to believe that Alan Sugar has room for any more entrepreneurial waifs and strays at this
point. But still, this reality perennial lumbers on as inevitably as tax returns. In this third
episode, expect a food fight as the contenders are given some basic ingredients and asked to turn
them into profit. _Phil Harrison_

FBI

9PM, SKY WITNESS
Paintings from a prestigious New York gallery have been stolen and a security guard left for dead
after a brazen heist. As Maggie and OA lead the team to recover the priceless artworks, they unearth
a far more dangerous plot behind the crime. Under mounting pressure, Maggie consults a friend for
help in profiling suspects. _Ali Catterall_

NOT WELCOME: THE BATTLE TO STOP THE BOATS

10PM, CHANNEL 4

[Not Welcome: The Battle to Stop the BoatsPictured: Calais]
Teenager Mustafa from Syria and transgender man Azzar from Saudi Arabia are two migrants telling the
stories of their crossings in this special programme. It also speaks to people who oppose them being
in the UK, including a single mother from Wales. _HR_
