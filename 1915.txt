TV tonight: more zombies get an ass-kicking in The Walking Dead spin-off

====================================================================================================

THE WALKING DEAD: DEAD CITY

9PM, SKY ONE
The spin-off from the zombie franchise that refuses to die is back for a third series – and
immediately gets back to what it does best: kicking infected ass in apocalyptic New York. Bitter
enemies Maggie (Lauren Cohan) and Negan (Jeffrey Dean Morgan) are at the point where Negan is
literally about to stab Maggie in the back – but an interruption brings a change in events …
_Hollie Richardson_

GEORGE CLARKE'S AMAZING SPACES: FIRST HOME

8PM, CHANNEL 4
This new series focuses on affordable, nontraditional homes. Even if it does accept the
impossibility of the housing market as a fait accompli, it offers intriguing ideas. This time: Kat,
whose bespoke tiny home will cost her £50k; Sean, who is building his own; and a Dutch experiment
in regulatory relaxation around construction. _Phil Harrison_

YOU'RE KILLING ME

8PM, U&DRAMA

[Two women stand side by side on a driveway looking concerned]
There are shades of Murder, She Wrote to this cosy, Maine-set whodunnit, as sleuthing novelist Allie
(Brooke Shields) and her gen Z sidekick Andi (Amalia Williamson) casually unmask killers. Their
latest case involves the suspicious suicide of a smarmy podcaster's wife. Can the duo poke holes in
his airtight alibi? _Graeme Virtue_

LIVE FROM THE LYCETT ARMS

10PM, CHANNEL 4
More boozy merriment as Joe Lycett's pop-up pub opens its doors once again. This time, landlord
Lycett will be delegating his least favourite tasks to temp manager Mel Giedroyc, while special
guest Sophie Willan lounges on a barstool and has a natter. As subliminal chatshow formats go, there
have been many worse. _PH_

NEW ZEALAND SPY

10PM, SKY ONE
The 1970s-set espionage spoof created by Kiwi comic Paul Williams wraps up its first season with a
deadpan double bill. With one hapless agent missing and the actually competent Sue (Rose Matafeo)
ready to quit, it's left to Michael (Williams) to don scuba gear and two wristwatches to foil a
toxic sabotage plot. _GV_

THE RAINMAKER

10.45PM, ITV1
"I guess you're right about being an acquired taste." Uh-oh. Is Rudy and Sarah's romance heading for
the rocks? Certainly, last week's expensive suit gift went down like a cup of cold sick, and episode
four puts their relationship under more pressure, when Sarah accidentally lets slip the existence of
the hospital's secret committee. _Ali Catterall_
