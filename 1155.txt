TV tonight: illicit affairs and revenge in a wonderful period drama

====================================================================================================

A WOMAN OF SUBSTANCE

9PM, CHANNEL 4
The rags-to-riches period drama continues with young Emma Harte (Jessica Reynolds) choosing a secret
relationship with her employer's son, Edwin Fairley (Ewan Horrocks), over taking the chance to move
to Leeds. It can't end well, though, as present-day Emma (Brenda Blethyn) still holds a grudge with
the Fairleys. It continues on Thursday with Lenny Rush giving a heartbreaking performance as Emma's
brother._Hollie Richardson_

THE MARLOW MURDER CLUB

8PM, U&DRAMA
When the much-beloved mayor of Marlow drops dead in the middle of a town council meeting, everyone
present is a suspect. It's the perfect locked-room mystery to challenge DS Tanika Malik's now
established crime-solving team, and the perfect series three opener for this cosy crime show._ Ellen
E Jones_

BEYOND THE BRUSH

8PM, SKY ARTS

[An early colour sketch of Munch's The Scream]
"This painting doesn't make any sense whatsoever." James Payne is an art historian who helps to tell
the story of Edvard Munch's The Scream, as part of a series that takes a closer look at well-known
paintings. Perhaps Munch's experience of his father's depression, mother's death and sister's
schizophrenia can explain the unsettling image. _HR_

HOSTAGE

9PM, BBC TWO
The final episode of this enthralling series about Islamic State kidnappings follows the ongoing
story of journalist John Cantlie. After his fellow hostage Jim Foley was beheaded, many in the UK
were shocked to see Cantlie parroting IS propaganda. Was he simply trying to survive? The issue is
explored carefully and with nuance. _Phil Harrison_

THE STOLEN GIRL

9PM, ITV1
Was it wise – or even ethical – to write an article suggesting that Elisa's (Denise Gough)
religious commune upbringing led to her daughter's kidnapping? Regardless, intrepid reporter Selma
(Ambika Mod) isn't backing down. She is determined to untangle the truth, and in this penultimate
episode she discovers a key link between Elisa and a fatal traffic accident. _EEJ_

WE MIGHT REGRET THIS

10PM, BBC TWO
The finale of a criminally short four-episode comedy series is a hen do from hell story (always good
fun, even if this one borders on excruciating). But while it causes Freya to question the whole
wedding, elsewhere Abe tells his ex-wife, Jane, that he's finally ready to sell their house. All the
while, the run-up to the big day is being sponsored on social media. _HR_
