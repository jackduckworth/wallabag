

====================================================================================================

TV tonight: has Richard E Grant blown it in The Celebrity Traitors?

====================================================================================================

THE CELEBRITY TRAITORS

8PM, BBC ONE
We've had our first round table – and incredibly frustrating cliffhanger ending – so now it's
time to find out which celebrity is the first to be banished. After confessing he is a traitor
minutes after being chosen, surely it will be Richard E Grant? But that's what's so delicious about
this game: you just never know what twist will come next. Continues on Friday with another murder.
_Hollie Richardson_

TASKMASTER

9.05PM, CHANNEL 4
Richard Ayoade's stagey (but convincing) disdain for the show he's agreed to appear on reaches new
heights as he threatens to destroy the whole set. Things aren't much better for the other
contenders, with Chloe Petts suffering a day of setbacks. No wonder Alex just wants to lie quietly
in a box for a while. _Phil Harrison_

ALL CREATURES GREAT AND SMALL

9.05PM, CHANNEL 5
While a stray pekingese needs a new home and Siegfried (Samuel West) becomes indignant with a car
mechanic, a trip out to help a stuck calf looks initially like standard hand-up-a-cow fare. But it's
a whopper! Is it time for the Dales' inaugural bovine caesarean? _Jack Seale_

NOT GOING OUT

9.30PM, BBC ONE
Nigel Planer cameos in Lee Mack's easy-going, long-running family sitcom this week. After seeing a
play together, Lee (Mack) and Lucy (Sally Bretton) bump into Nigel in a fancy restaurant. Cue a
night of awkward introductions, farcical mix-ups and a few laughs. _HR_

BREAK CLAUSE

[Kira is holding a baby, whom Lil is admiring, in a cloakroom]
10.05PM, CHANNEL 4
More from the housing crisis comedy, where a couple split up on their first night in London but are
stuck in a six-month contract. After Lil's (Lara Ricote) excruciating first rebound date, she and
Ben (Samuel Bottomley) head back to Leeds for their mates' baby's christening – the perfect
opportunity for Lil to find the woman with whom Ben cheated on her. _HR_

THE PAPER

11PM, SKY ONE
The Office spin-off prints out an efficient double bill to conclude season two. Editor Ned (Domhnall
Gleeson) is still trying to land his haymaker investigative piece, but should he and reporter Mare
(Chelsea Frei) prioritise love over journalism? The finale has a nice amount of weakness for
open-hearted emotion. _JS_
