TV tonight: a new mismatched detective duo for crime drama fans

====================================================================================================

WINTER

9PM, ITV1
If you have room for one more mismatched detective duo in your life, this new drama introduces Dr
Ethan Winter (Richard Armitage), an unbearably smug forensic pathologist who can "read a body like a
book", and DI Lauren Bell (Annabel Scholey), a diligent, frosty cop. The pair are on a collision
course as the murder of a young woman lands in their in trays. But when an explosive postmortem
raises the stakes, they are forced to tolerate each other. _Phil Harrison_

THE SHAMAN'S GRAVE

8PM, BBC FOUR
The capacity of archaeology to open a window into human belief systems is at the heart of this
documentary exploring a very cold case from Germany. What do the bodies of a stone age woman and
child – and the other items in their shared grave – tell us about the era's spiritual practices?
_PH_

CAUSE OF DEATH

9PM, CHANNEL 5
Nineteen-year-old Jay Slater was holidaying in Tenerife when he tripped and fell to his death at the
bottom of a ravine. Now, pathologist Dr Richard Shepherd returns to the scene of the disaster with
Jay's mum, Debbie, recreating his last walk in order to work out what killed him: deyhdration, a
lack of sleep or something else? _Priya Elan_

YUJA

9PM, SKY ARTS
This intriguing portrait of the prodigious Chinese-American pianist Yuja Wang does a good job of
telling her life story while leaving some of her essential mystery intact. She's a charming but
abstracted interviewee: reclining, as if in therapy, and struggling to fully explain her brilliance.
_PH_

NOT GOING OUT

9.30PM, BBC ONE

[A man with a beard wearing a suit jacket and a woman in black with a jacket over her arm holding a
phone speak to another man with stubble, wearing a grey hoodie and checked pyjama bottoms]
There are shades of the Fawlty Towers anniversary episode as Lee (Lee Mack) desperately tries to
deflect withering house guests Anna and Toby (Abigail Cruttenden and Hugh Dennis) away from the
discovery that Lucy (Sally Bretton) is hiding from them upstairs. The cat doesn't help. _Jack Seale_

MARTIN COMPSTON: LIVING LAS VEGAS

10PM, CHANNEL 4
"Now I know why John Wayne walked like that …" Affable tour guide Compston saddles up for an
authentic cowboy adventure in the second instalment of his Sin City travelogue. Other highlights
include a raucous cocktail sesh with Scottish drag star Lawrence Chaney and a belated trip to the
Grand Canyon. _Graeme Virtue_
