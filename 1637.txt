Taylor Swift May Have Done Something That Could Change Her Whole Identity. Get Ready to Roll Your Eyes.

====================================================================================================

I don’t regret losing my mind a little over Taylor Swift’s recent wedding, nor should you. We
all deserve to have a little fun. But I do think it may be time to move on, at least until some
photos emerge. Luckily, a new Taylor Swift–related thing to obsess over has arrived just in time
to fill the Madison Square Garden–sized void in our collective hivemind: the question of whether
Swift, a longtime cat devotee, has a secret dog.

On Monday, a fluffy white dog was photographed disembarking from Swift’s jet along with Swift’s
husband, Travis Kelce, in Palm Beach, Florida. If you haven’t taken a gander at the photos yet,
you really ought to, because the presumably covertly taken image of a fancy, snowy puppy padding
down a billionaire’s private plane’s airstair is exactly as pleasingly silly as it sounds.
Tabloid outlets like TMZ and Page Six published the photos, as did the Instagram gossip account Deux
Moi, which memorably referred to the pet as the couple’s “alleged dog,” a phrase that
highlights the absurdity of the situation: This couple is so famous that even the furry friends in
their vicinity are not safe from the prying eyes of the public. This couple is so famous that they
are being accused of hiding a dog the way some politicians and famous people hide secret children.
This couple is so famous that perhaps it’s not out of the question that the alleged dog isn’t a
dog at all—who knows what they’re capable of, you know?

Or maybe they just got a dog. Though the wider world has been focused on the wedding and all the
attendant speculation about its location and scope, more dialed-in fans have known and been
wondering about the dog for several months. In March, a Swift fan account on X posted footage of
presumably the same white dog exiting an SUV that is thought to be owned by Swift. This followed a
February podcast appearance in which Kelce’s brother Jason revealed that Travis had just gotten a
puppy. Surprisingly, no one talked about it all that much, maybe due to a combination of denial and
there being so much else to discuss with the wedding approaching. But theories about the couple’s
dog ownership really began to spread post-wedding, thanks to a blurry photo from the event that
showed up in a Daily Mail leak: Among the decor at Madison Square Garden was a framed photo in which
Swift appeared to be cuddling a white dog beside Kelce.

I mean, it kind of seems like they just got a dog, as long-term millennial couples are wont to do.
While it makes sense that they wouldn’t send out a press release about it, it also seems a little
weird to actively keep it a secret. Some observers have pointed to a late-2025 episode of _New
Heights_, Travis’ podcast with Jason, as evidence that a nefarious dog-hiding plot is afoot:
Travis mentioned a special holiday gift he had received from Swift, but declined to elaborate,
saying, “it gives away things that we’re trying to keep in-house, so not gonna say it.” Though
Page Six reported that the couple has had the dog for closer to a year, I’m not sure what else
would have prompted Kelce to be so cagey. Weird, right? It’s hard to imagine why anyone would need
to keep a dog “in-house” … until you think about it for a single second and remember how
insane everybody is about these people, us very much included. (Still, dog or not, that is one
well-trained man—now I am imagining him and the dog in obedience school together.)

Though Swift has owned dogs before, over the years she’s really leaned into her identity as a cat
person. She owns three, and she posed with one of them when she was on the cover of Time magazine as
its person of the year. When she endorsed Kamala Harris for president in 2024, she referred to
herself as a “childless cat lady,” a dig at some comments from then-candidate for vice president
J.D. Vance. For anyone inclined to complain about how Swift has changed since beginning her
relationship with Travis, perhaps her getting a dog with him lends credence to that narrative. Some
observers criticized what they perceived to be a strain of conservatism, tradwifery even, on
Swift’s most recent album. Is having a dog the ultimate dog whistle? No, obviously, but you can
understand why Swift might want to circumvent that conversation. Or maybe Kelce wanted to save the
reveal until he could monetize it: There is some speculation online, again thanks to Deux Moi, that
the dog will be part of an upcoming Tommy Hilfiger campaign Kelce shot.

Whether the dog stars in an ad or not, it seems inevitable that he or she is about to get pretty
famous and ought to get used to the attention. The consensus seems to be that it’s a Samoyed. Some
quick research tells me that Samoyeds have occasionally gone viral for “singing” on pitch, so
maybe we can expect one to show up on a future Swift track. People on the Samoyed subreddit are
interested but think whoever groomed this one cut its hair way too short. Swift’s cats are named
Olivia Benson, Meredith Grey, and Benjamin Button, so I’m sure there will be betting markets
trying to guess its name, if there aren’t already. My guess? Adam Sandler.
