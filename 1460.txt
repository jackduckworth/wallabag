TV tonight: zooming in on 10 iconic David Beckham photos

====================================================================================================

BECKHAM: IN TEN PICTURES

9PM, BBC TWO
"He knows he's going to be photographed; he knows he's going to cause a sensation." A clever concept
to profile people in new ways, this time focusing on Becks. It starts in 1986 with a photographer
who, after hearing that his teenage nephew's mate has just won a national football skills
competition, goes to capture his triumph. From there, the photo album includes a sarong, a Spice
Girl and a gay magazine cover. _Hollie Richardson_

REPORTED MISSING

9PM, BBC ONE
It's the stuff of a thriller movie in the documentary series about Police Scotland's efforts to find
missing persons. The alarm is raised about a Chinese student who has vanished from Edinburgh, yet
when the cops track him down in Durham, he says he's fine. That's just the beginning of a story
driven by lies, deceit and CCTV clips. Also, a hiker is lost in the mountains. _Jack Seale_

TASKMASTER

9PM, CHANNEL 4
We're into the closing stretch of one of the more enjoyable recent editions, and four contestants
can realistically still hope to win Greg Davies's golden noggin. Among the many delights has been
the descent into goofiness of eminent screenwriter Armando Iannucci, although as he shows tonight,
he can turn cranky when riled. _Phil Harrison_

THE HARDACRES

9PM, CHANNEL 5

[A man in a red military uniform holding the hand of a woman in an evening dress]
The moreish period drama about the gutsy Hardacre family continues with a ball for their staff. Liza
and Edward get engaged, which leaves Adella feeling alone. Meanwhile, Harry is devastated to learn
that his tutor is leaving soon. Will any of this spoil the fun and frolics of the big night? _HR_

PRISONER

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC
Can we expect the final episode of this excellent dark crime drama to tie up all the loose ends?
After last week's shock death and arrest, it's not looking likely that Tibor and Amber will make it
to court on time and manage to bring down the crime syndicate Pegasus. _Priya Elan_

MAKE THAT MOVIE

10PM, CHANNEL 4
The brilliant Amy Gledhill guest-stars in Sam Campbell's comedy about a director who brings ordinary
people's film ideas to life. She plays a teacher whose pupils pitch "Bog Prom" in dedication to her
(she's an expert on the local mummified bog man and didn't go to her prom). Crew member Sebastian
(Aaron Chen) continues to be the standout. _HR_
