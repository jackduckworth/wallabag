

====================================================================================================

One of the Olympics’ Biggest Stars Is at the Center of a Bizarre Intra-Squad Drama

====================================================================================================

_Olympics Jerk Watch__ is a long-running feature that subjectively rates the jerkiness (or lack
thereof) of the Games’ biggest stars. Read more of Slate’s 2026 Olympics coverage __here__._

NOMINEE: Julia Simon

HOME COUNTRY: France

KNOWN FOR: Winning biathlon medals, stealing a teammate’s credit card, not taking full
responsibility for stealing a teammate’s credit card

WHY SHE MIGHT BE A JERK: Julia Simon is one of the top female biathletes in Europe, which makes her
a figure of some renown on a continent where biathlon is the most popular winter sport. At the Milan
Cortina Games, she lived up to her reputation, winning three gold medals and one silver. Before
these Winter Games began, though, Simon made headlines for her performance in a very different
field: internecine credit-card fraud.

In 2022, while the French team was in Norway for a summer biathlon festival, Justine Braisaz-Bouchet
noticed that someone had used her credit card to make more than $2,000 worth of online purchases.
Getting a fraud alert is bad enough. But to make matters worse, Braisaz-Bouchet soon realized that
the purchases had apparently been made by her biathlon teammate and longtime rival: Simon. Ooh la
la!

Braisaz-Bouchet filed charges in 2023. At the same time, Simon was having a banner year that ended
with her winning the Crystal Globe, which is awarded by the International Biathlon Union to the
season’s best biathlete. French biathlon fans didn’t necessarily want to hear that their biggest
star was perhaps also a thief, and Braisaz-Bouchet has said that “for many people I was the
troublemaker.” Silent complicity in victim-blaming is pretty jerky.

Simon initially claimed that she was innocent, speculating that the _real_ thief had perhaps stolen
_her_ identity as a form of camouflage, much like how O.J. Simpson was framed by the real killer.
But further investigation revealed that Simon had _also_ allegedly made unauthorized purchases with
a team staffer’s credit card. In October 2025, she was found guilty in a French court and was
given a three-month suspended sentence and a five-figure fine. “I can’t explain it. I don’t
remember doing it. I can’t make sense of it,” she said in court. Chalking it up to amnesiais
something that only true jerks do!

After Simon’s conviction, the French ski federation handed her a six-month ban, five months of
which were conveniently suspended until after the Olympics. That means she’ll be in real big
trouble if she steals _another_ teammate’s credit card between now and mid-May. In effect, Simon
got away scot-free, which seems like a pretty jerky outcome. After all, when Ruben Rivera stole
Derek Jeter’s mitt out of his locker during spring training and sold it to a memorabilia dealer
for $2,500, he was kicked off the New York Yankees and never appeared in the big leagues again. Now,
nobody’s saying that Simon deserved to be kicked off the Yankees, too. (That would be crazy, the
Yankees are counting on her this year!) But shouldn’t her ban have been substantial enough to stop
her from competing in the Olympics?

The difference here, I suppose, is that Rivera has a .216 career batting average, while Simon is a
star. If _Jeter_ had stolen _Rivera’s_ glove, you can bet that the Yankees would’ve allowed
their captain to compete in the World Series and/or the Olympic biathlon.

Similarly, the French ski federation didn’t want to sideline one of the nation’s medal hopefuls.
They’ve been rewarded for that decision: As previously mentioned, Simon won four medals in the
2026 Games, two of them in team events. Has all that success for the French squad translated into
_bonhomie_? “The page has been turned within the team—we have spoken about it,” Simon told
Eurosport. Sounds convincing to me.

In her public-facing appearances during the 2026 Games, Simon hasn’t seemed particularly
repentant. To the contrary: After crossing the finish line in the women’s 15-kilometer individual
event, she held her finger to her lips in a “shushing” motion, later saying in a press
conference that the gesture “was for one person, he knows it. We had a talk, so I won’t [say]
anything more about it. Yeah, it’s over now.” Kind of a jerk move not to tell the media who that
“one person” is!

WHY SHE MIGHT NOT BE A JERK: Although I mocked Simon’s “fugue state” defense in the preceding
section, I suppose there is a world in which it holds water. Simon was in her early- to mid-20s when
the offenses occurred. Sometimes, when young athletes face intense competitive pressures, they
respond to those pressures in strange and unaccountable ways.

It is certainly possible that Simon wasn’t in her right mind when she committed these crimes.
Simon has said that she’s been working with a therapist to try and understand what she did and why
she did it. We might consider taking her words at face value: If these incidents _were_ the result
of some sort of untreated psychological problem, then maybe Simon deserves compassion moreso than
our reflexive scorn. It would probably help her cause, though, if she didn’t make mocking hand
gestures. Gesture-related mockery is the known province of jerks!

It could be that biathletes are just wired this way. I already wrote about the Norwegian who stole
his victorious teammate’s thunder by blurting out in a post-race interview that he had cheated on
his girlfriend and was desperate to win her back. That guy, Sturla Holm Lægreid, finished the 2026
Games with _five_ medals, one more than Simon. Way to show up _this_ jerk, you jerk!

Also, last year another member of the French biathlon team was caught tampering with her
teammate’s rifle, in what the Independent called “a gross attempt to steal points at [the
teammate’s] expense.” Who knows, maybe there’s just something about biathlon that attracts
these kinds of people?

Well, at least they don’t have guns! (Takes a five-minute break to learn more about biathlon.)
Well, at least they only have guns part of the time!

JERK SCORE: As a reminder, we score our jerk candidates on style, technical merit, and execution.
I’ll give Julia Simon 2.5 out of 3 points for style, because it would have been slightly jerkier
for her to make a “swiping a credit card” gesture upon victoriously crossing the finish line. 2
out of 3 points for technical merit, because a Norwegian summer biathlon festival sounds like a very
pleasant venue for credit-card fraud. 2 out of 3 points for execution, because she could have also
stolen her teammate’s traveler’s checks. And 1 out of 1 points because it was very jerky of her
not to buy me anything with her teammate’s credit card. 7.5 out of 10 points for Julia Simon.
