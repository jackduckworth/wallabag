AI MODE REPLY FOR WALLABAG "PLAIN TEXT" FORBIDDEN

When Wallabag shows"PLAIN TEXT"along with a"FORBIDDEN"(403) error or message for a saved article, it
means[Highlight start] Wallabag's fetcher (Graby) was blocked by the target website when trying to
download the full HTML article [Highlight end] . Instead of getting a clean article body, it fell
back to raw/plain text output containing the site's 403 block page or redirect message. 
Why This Happens

	* ANTI-SCRAPING / BOT PROTECTION: Many sites or CDNs (like Cloudflare) block server-side requests
from datacenter or VPS IPs.

	* COOKIE GATES OR LOGIN WALLS: Sites that rely on cookies, redirects, or authentication may reject
headless/server fetches.

	* OUTDATED SITE RULES: A site's layout or protection rules may need updated Graby site
configuration. 

How to Work Around or Fix It

	* USE THE WALLABAGGER BROWSER EXTENSION: Enable "RETRIEVE CONTENT FROM THE BROWSER" in the
extension settings so your browser fetches the page with your session/cookies and sends the content
to Wallabag instead of letting your server fetch it directly.

	* RE-FETCH AFTER UPDATES: Once site configs or Wallabag/Graby updates are applied, click RE-FETCH
CONTENT on the saved entry.

	* CHECK LOGS IF SELF-HOSTING: Inspect var/logs/prod.log (or enable var/logs/graby.log for debug
details) to confirm whether the remote server returned a 403 or redirect loop. 

See thewallabag fetch errors documentationfor detailed log and site-config troubleshooting steps.
