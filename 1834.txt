TV tonight: join the jury as a teenager goes on trial for murder

====================================================================================================

THE JURY: 14 AND ON TRIAL FOR MURDER

9PM, CHANNEL 4 
With trial by jury under scrutiny, this is a timely moment for the return of this gripping series,
which reruns an actual criminal trial and follows the 12 jury members as they reach their verdict.
This time, the case of brothers – one of whom is only 14 years old – accused of killing their
stepfather causes instant polarisation. Debates around nature and nurture and punishment for its own
sake emerge straight away. _Phil__ Harrison_

RACE AGAINST THE TIDE

7.30PM, BBC TWO 
This good vibes sand-sculpting contest should really be called the Great British Scrape Off. With no
eliminations last time, the six teams gather in St Andrews to tackle their latest challenge:
smoothing a beach into something that evokes the beauty and motion of the ocean. _Graeme Virtue_

THE GREAT BRITISH SEWING BEE

8PM, BBC ONE

[Judges Esme Young and Patrick Grant in The Great British Sewing Bee.]
A deerstalker fit for Sherlock Holmes; an outfit for a grownup Matilda made out of old school
uniforms; a made-to-measure Regency dress inspired by the world of Jane Austen – it can only be
literary icons week in the sewing competition. Who will make it to the next chapter? _Hollie
Richardson_

CELEBRITY MASTERCHEF

9PM, BBC ONE 
This week, the competitors include plum-voiced prankster Max Fosh, who does lots of unintentional
Partridge ("Bosh!"); Una from the Saturdays, who likes to talk about potatoes; and Paralympian
Lauren Steadman, who says she forces everyone she cooks for to give her marks out of 10 for the
food. Sounds relaxing. _Priya Elan_

THE GAME

9PM, CHANNEL 5
The excellent Jason Watkins returns as embattled former detective Huw Miller in the second series of
this somewhat hysterical thriller. Huw and his wife, Alice, have retired to a remote coastal home,
but the past hasn't left them behind as letters start to arrive from Robson Green's imprisoned
serial killer, Patrick Harbottle. _PH_

LITTLE BIRD

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC

[A woman stands in a kitchen looking distressed.]
It's the concluding double bill of this deeply moving drama about the Sixties Scoop in Canada, when
Indigenous children were taken from their families. After reuniting with her twin brother,
Esther/Bezhig receives tragic news in the finale – and it brings the Birds back together after so
many years. _H__R_
