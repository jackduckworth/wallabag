TV tonight: Michael Sheen exposes a toxic scandal in north Wales

====================================================================================================

BURIED WITH MICHAEL SHEEN

9PM, BBC TWO
There's a lagoon in Ruabon, north Wales, that is so toxic that the barrels containing the chemicals
dumped in it have rotted away. Until about 20 years ago, children used to play around it. Michael
Sheen's compassionate and enraging exposé of the alleged poisoning of parts of his homeland decades
ago by contractors working for the now defunct chemical company Monsanto concludes with the actor
meeting people with cancer who are searching for accountability. Spoiler alert: it's an uphill
battle. _Phil Harrison_

MARTIN & ROMAN'S IRISH ROAD TRIP

8PM, CHANNEL 4
The final leg of the father and son's tour takes them to a snowy Northern Ireland. They spend a
night in Ballygally Castle – said to be the most haunted hotel in the country – play a game of
hurling, vampire-hunt, and swim in the freezing sea. You can't accuse them of not getting stuck in.
_Hollie Richardson_

MY LIFE IS MURDER

8PM, U&ALIBI
More low-stakes antipodean crime-solving fun with Lucy Lawless as investigator Alexa Crowe. This
time, she deals with the case of a wealthy woman who is killed amid a dog custody dispute. She finds
herself in the bizarre world of millionaire dating, before racing to save another pet victim. _HR_

LONG LOST FAMILY: BORN WITHOUT TRACE – WHAT HAPPENED NEXT

9PM, ITV1
The lovely but devastating show speaks to another pair of foundling siblings: Natasha and Lee-Ann
want to know who left them as babies, two years apart, in the toilets of two different south London
hospitals. And nurse Liz updates on what has happened since she shared her story of being found
severely ill as a baby in a hedge. _HR_

CELEBRITY HUNTED

9PM, CHANNEL 4

[Scarlette Douglas smiling as she swims wearing a headscarf and cold water gear]
Celebrity status adds a different dimension to this entertaining if always mildly sinister
nationwide game of hide and seek. Is it really possible to blend in with the civilians? In this
penultimate episode, one of our celebrities makes a basic error and seeks refuge with a known
contact in Scotland. Oops. _PH_

SECRET LIVES OF GYPSY WIVES

10PM, CHANNEL 4
The buzzy reality show starring Gypsy influencer Trewley Precious ends its first season on a high
with the social media star celebrating her 26th birthday in typically boisterous style. But there is
also an emotive wedding dress fitting for her sister Pearly and a potentially problematic brand
partnership to consider. _Graeme Virtue_

FILM CHOICE

[Helen Schlegel (Helena Bonham Carter) with Leonard Bast (Samuel West) in Howards End]
HOWARDS END (JAMES IVORY, 1992), 1.15PM, FILM4
EM Forster's 1910 novel about class, money, love and a house in the country was styled into a
prestige period drama by Merchant Ivory, aided by Ruth Prawer Jhabvala's Oscar-winning script. Three
levels of society come into conflict in a story of chance encounters, misguided charity and
patriarchal hypocrisy. Anthony Hopkins's rigid, rich businessman, Henry Wilcox, is drawn to the
bohemian, privileged Schlegel sisters, Margaret (Emma Thompson) and Helen (Helena Bonham Carter),
while they get involved in the life of a poor clerk, Leonard Bast (Samuel West). _Simon Wardell_

NIGHTCRAWLER (DAN GILROY, 2014), 9PM, SKY ATLANTIC
It may have dated somewhat in this age of people filming everything on their phones, but Dan
Gilroy's dark 2014 drama is still wholly relevant in its clear-eyed critique of the public's
obsession with true crime. Jake Gyllenhaal – hollow of eye and creepy of smile – plays Louis, a
sociopathic petty thief who finds a new job driving around LA shooting graphic footage of police
incidents for the local TV news. If it bleeds it leads, with his ratings-chasing boss, Nina (Rene
Russo), encouraging Louis' worst tendencies. _SW_
