TV tonight: a battle of the lawyers in this glossy John Grisham series

====================================================================================================

THE RAINMAKER

9PM, ITV1
This glossy legal drama, a hit in the US last year, is based on John Grisham's novel. Set in
Charleston, it follows ambitious young couple Rudy (Milo Callaghan) and Sarah (Madison Iseman) who
are associates at the esteemed Tinley Britt. But Leo is fired after a run-in with John Slattery's
smarmy boss Leo – and he takes a job at a slightly less, let's say, refined firm. Cue a battle of
the lawyers. _Hollie Richardson_

THE MET ORCHESTRA PLAYS STRAUSS AT THE PROMS

7PM, BBC FOUR
A Proms debut as New York's Met Orchestra take their place on the Royal Albert Hall stage for the
first time, under the baton of music director Yannick Nézet-Séguin. They'll be playing a programme
of music by Richard Strauss – highlights include the final scene of Salome sung by soprano Elza
van den Heever. _Phil Harrison_

GARDENERS' WORLD

8PM, BBC TWO
Burn after weeding: the scorching summer has been a punishing one for many gardens, but Monty Don is
on hand with advice on how to help water-starved plants and lawns recover. Other horticultural
highlights include an update on Rekha Mistry's attempts to harvest fruit and veg from pots on her
compact patio. _Graeme Virtue_

YOU'RE KILLING ME

8PM, U&DRAMA

[A crowd of guests, male and female, at a wedding ceremony, with the sea behind them]
The screwball arguments make this show much funnier than the average cosy-crime comedy drama.
Episode two sees novelist Allie (Brooke Shields) and podcaster Andi (Amalia Williamson), established
as a reluctant writing/crime-solving partnership, looking into a death at a wedding while bickering
magnificently. _Jack Seale_

FONTAINES DC @ READING FESTIVAL 2026

10.40PM, BBC ONE
Grian Chatten's Dublin post-punkers seemed unlikely headlining material when they emerged almost a
decade ago. But they've developed the visual side of their shows, written some anthemic bangers and
gathered an unusually devoted legion of fans. This set kicks off a weekend of coverage of the
pre-A-levels musical extravaganza_. PH_

GRIMSBURG

MIDNIGHT, SKY ONE
In this season finale, Marvin is suffering from a serious case of PTSD and keeps seeing murder
everywhere, but the real mystery is who stole his Friday treat danish? With gags about Tom Petty and
gluten intolerance, this snappy, Jon Hamm-voiced parody of unreconstructed alpha cops has been a
hoot. _Priya Elan_
