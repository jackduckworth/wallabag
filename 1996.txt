TV tonight: Vanessa Williams sits for this wonderful portrait competition

====================================================================================================

PORTRAIT ARTIST OF THE YEAR 2026

8PM, SKY ARTS
Stressful or soothing? It's always a fine line between the two with this portrait competition. Art
historian Katy Hessel joins the judging team for the show's 13th series, with Stephen Mangan
resuming hosting duties. The first three sitters are actors Lennie James, Sheridan Smith and Vanessa
Williams. Who will break down in (happy) tears, telling a painter: "You've captured something I
thought I'd lost"?_ Hollie Richardson_

ECHOES FROM BELOW: EXPLORING BRITAIN'S CAVES

8PM, BBC FOUR
Titan is the deepest cave shaft ever found in Britain. A gutsy scanning team head in there in an
attempt to find out exactly how large it is – and it might even be too big for them to manage. The
programme also looks at other cave discoveries, including the 17th-century "witches' marks" at
Creswell Crags. _HR_

WINTER

9PM, ITV1
Richard Armitage probes corpses for clues as maverick Bristol pathologist Dr Ethan Winter, a man
troubled by the murder of his fiancee and by the similarity of this show's premise to countless
others. As a brutal murder proves not to be all it seems, Winter's hunt for the truth gets risky._
Jack Seale_

GRAND DESIGNS

9PM, CHANNEL 4
Living in an old driving test centre felt like a good idea when Edinburgh artists Jana and Rory
first had the opportunity. But the freezing winters have tempered their enthusiasm. Can they turn
the site into a modern home on a limited budget? And can their kids live in a caravan while they
try? _Phil Harrison_

CAUSE OF DEATH

9PM, CHANNEL 5
A sad and shocking look at the unexpected death of 16-year-old Danny Nicholls, who was found
unresponsive on the living room floor by his sister. A medical investigation showed a hidden reason
that could have suddenly stopped his heart from beating. _HR_

MARTIN COMPSTON: LIVING LAS VEGAS

10PM, CHANNEL 4
From Greenock to Vegas, baby! Line of Duty's Martin Compston relocated to America's playground when
he married a local. In this episode, he brings his Tom Cruise-like glee to pole dancing, flying in a
helicopter across the strip and meeting the "king of Vegas", singer Wayne Newton, who at 84 is still
going strong. _Priya Elan_
