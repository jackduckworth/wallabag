TV tonight: can a breakup in a flatshare ever end well?

====================================================================================================

BREAK CLAUSE

10.05PM, CHANNEL 4
Can a breakup in a flatshare end well? In the closing double bill, Ben (Samuel Bottomley) and Lil
(Lara Ricote) are single and mingling – but they also find themselves back in each other's arms,
gallivanting around London … before reality hits again. With their six-month break clause in the
lease coming up, they need to decide whether to get back together or move on for good. _Hollie
Richardson_

THE CELEBRITY TRAITORS

8PM, BBC ONE 

Myha'la and Julie are banished, while Hannah is still – somehow – in the game. Does this mean
that, after a rocky start with Richard E Grant, the traitors are starting to get good? Tonight we
find out how the game of hide and seek turns out, and whether Leigh-Anne Pinnock's shield means that
no one gets murdered. _HR_

OUR TINY ISLANDS

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
This series, looking at more than 400 small isles round the UK coast, visits a sheep farmer on Iona
in the Hebrides with a unique wool product, and the Lundy Company, which allows residents of the
Bristol Channel island to keep living there. Plus, a look at the gardening upkeep of a 3.8 hectare
(9.5 acre) croft in North Uist – so brace for a bucolic watch that might inspire a digital detox._
Alexi Duggins_

THE YORKSHIRE VET

8PM, CHANNEL 5

[A woman with a yellow vest top smiles as she hugs a very fluffy brown and grey cat with blue eyes.]

Peter faces a desperate case when a newborn lamb arrives with a rare birth defect: "It hasn't got a
bottom." Meanwhile, Shona treats Grace, a shire horse with a uterine infection; Rohin rescues an
injured pheasant; and Julian and Lucy give Frank Sinatra, a ragdoll cat, a much-needed makeover:
"What do you think about a mohawk?" _Ali Catterall_

CLASSIC MOVIES: THE STORY OF A MATTER OF LIFE AND DEATH

8PM, SKY ARTS 
With its tragically romantic opening scene – a second world war pilot chatting up a radio operator
as his broken plane plummets – Powell and Pressburger's strange, celestial wartime drama is a
beloved classic. Here, critics gleefully dig into exactly why that is. _HR_

ALL CREATURES GREAT AND SMALL

9.05PM, CHANNEL 5 
Another testosterone-filled, action-packed episode of the veterinary period drama: Tristan has a
bargain haircut from Josh for his final date with Charlotte before she heads off to New York. Good
idea or not? Then absolute calamity: a dog named Venus and a pet rat called Bunthorne both go
walkabout. And there's a disagreement at Heston over a cow. Cripes. _AC_
