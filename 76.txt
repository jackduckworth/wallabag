Guard At Tomb Of The Unknown Soldier Puts Secretary Sh*tfaced In His Place
He thinks he cares about the military, which makes his stewardship of it even more of a joke. But
the military he cares about is the one he’s invented in his booze-damaged brain, and he views that
make-believe military as a plaything. It’s not the real military, the one that never considered
him all that impressive. That military, the real one, is where Whiskeydick honed his instincts
blaming everybody but himself for his own pathetic mediocrity. That’s the military that made him
jealous when it gave a woman a Silver Star for her actions in Iraq based on what he called “an
agenda,” and you know what Pete doesn’t have? A Silver Star.

Pete has made his entire personality about projecting his silly masculine insecurities on to the
military writ large, blaming the US’s losses in Iraq, Afghanistan, and every other war since Korea
on it being, in the words of Thomas Massie this week, too “fat and gay” and not _tuff enough_,
instead of being man enough and smart enough to grapple with the notion that stupid warmongering
policies are why the US hasn’t won a war in decades. (And now, under Pete, we’re losing them
faster, and in more humiliating fashion!)

But then there are moments that really drive home how little Secretary Shitfaced cares about the
sacrifices of the men and women he doesn’t deserve to serve alongside, much less command, and one
of those was yesterday, when the bastard motherfuckers and his griftybabysitter wife and his garbage
aides had to be loudly scolded by the guard on duty at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, because they
were too busy yukking it up to show respect.

“ALL VISITORS WILL MAINTAIN AN ATMOSPHERE OF SILENCE AND RESPECT AT ALL TIMES!” the guard
shouted, to the Fox News weekend host who the bootlicking Republicans in the Senate confirmed to run
the most powerful military the world has ever known, who was giggling with his entourage and taking
selfies.

Today’s vocabulary word is _detritus_. The people getting yelled at in that video are _detritus_.

unbelievably high honor of guarding the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, an unbroken, 24/7 vigil since
1937. As she notes, small children can figure out that this is not a place to misbehave — there
are signs that serve as helpful reminders, if you’re dense! — but the ne’er-do-well credibly
accused sexual predator Pete Hegseth isn’t capable of understanding it, and nor is his garbage
wife or the people he surrounds himself with. (Specifically he was with that aforementioned wife,
Jennifer, plus Chief of Staff Ricky Buria, Deputy Chief of Staff Patrick Weaver, and Senior Adviser
Tami Radabaugh.)

We encourage you to read what Clymer wrote about the process of becoming one of the Sentinels of the
