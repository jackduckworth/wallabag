If The Question Is ‘Is Tommy Tuberville Really That Stupid?’ The Answer Is Always Yes

====================================================================================================

In the great manufactured MAGA tantrum about men in women’s sports and kids becoming trans by
watching Ms. Rachel, or whatever they’re hootin’ and hollerin’ about today, it can be really
fun to fuck with these bigots, because they often don’t even know what they’re saying or what
they believe. They just know their grunting hatred has been directed toward trans kids, so they
start their engines and they grunt, hatefully.

Do they believe that kids are being allowed to use different pronouns at school without necessarily
telling their parents, or do they believe the school nurse is doing top surgery during recess?
It’s hard to tell, because of what idiots they sound like talking about it. Did y’all see the
woman in the _New York Times_ focus group about the Texas Senate race who didn’t want to vote for
James Talarico because he believes in “same-sex bathrooms in high school and grade school”?

Sit with that one for a minute. That’s what we mean when we say these people are just mad barking
idiots at this point, babbling hate in whatever direction they’ve been trained to. They literally
do not know what they’re mad at.

We’ve just gotten through a couple weeks of making fun of Stupidest Republican Senator in history
Tommy Tuberville, who appears to truly believe that the Venn diagram between airline pilots and
furries (a concept he thoroughly does not understand) is one great big circle. When everybody
started making fun of him, he doubled down, shoutin’ up and down that he heard about pilots
barking and meowing at each other. Yet again, Tommy Tuberville showed that he was completely
confused about something he encountered in the real world, and that it scared him.

Does Tommy Tuberville think the school nurse is doing top surgery, and maybe bottom surgery? Is
Tommy Tuberville so stupid that he can’t come at that question from MULTIPLE directions, like a
normal human with functioning brain might, to determine that people are pullin’ his leg? Yes, yes,
yes, yes, and yes. No matter the question, if it revolves around “Is Tommty Tuberville that
stupid?” the answer is always yes.

This is not a powerful propagandist, this is one of their paste-eating, dumbfuck bumpkin marks. He
just happens to be a powerful US senator, and likely soon a governor of an entire US state.

Tubblety Bobbity Boo explained this weekend, as the opening act at a Trump rally:

> “I’m not even going to get into the transgender nonsense because y’all know how I feel about
> men in women’s sports. But what gets me is the Biden administration — up until President Trump
> — allowed doctors to cut on minors without telling their parents. [_Crowd boos_.] Think about
> that.

Yeah we’re thinking about it, and there are so many red flags that should lead a barely sentient
person to say “Wait, what?” Like for one thing, huge screaming red flag, _health insurance_. Is
there no process of co-pays and insurance claims with “Biden cuttin’ on minors’ peeners”
surgery? Does nobody have to check to make sure the doctor is in network? Is that the _one kind_ of
healthcare that can’t bankrupt people in the United States, because of how free it is? Is there
some kind of carve-out in Obamacare? Wouldn’t Tommy Tuberville, as a US senator, know that sort of
information, or have access to it?

Christ, what a fucking dumbass.

> “You talk about government control. Can you imagine if your kid would come home one day and says
> — what’s wrong with you? — ‘Well, I’m taking these drugs. And I’m getting ready to
> have surgery because my teachers and my principal says I can.’ Folks, we’ve lost our damn
> mind. We really have.”

Yeah, somebody’s lost their mind, that’s for sure.

“I’m getting ready to have surgery, Mom and Dad. It’s a school thing.”

Didn’t even require a permission slip.

Or it did, but you know kids these days, forging their mom’s signature.

Goddammit, what a stupid piece of shit.

That’s it, that is your post, we hope we don’t have to write a _third_ followup on this, but we
probably will. Tommy Tuberville is such a babbling pigfuck he thinks kids have surgery without
telling their parents. That’s brain damage right there. Or God put it together wrong in the first
place.

Somebody should’ve sent little Tommy Tuberville to the school nurse, for some goddamned brain
surgery.
