====================================================================================================

EVOLUTION

9PM, BBC TWO
Which animal was the first to poo? Chris Packham's belting biology lesson continues with the story
of the bat – and the origins of its insatiable hunger are in the urbilaterian, an animal that
floated about in the waters of the Ediacaran period, about 570m years ago. Food entered and exited
through one hole … until a vital mutation: "The anus was a breakthrough!" _Hollie Richardson_

THE GHETTOS & THE HOLOCAUST

11.50AM, PBS AMERICA
"Today, the Jewish ghetto was officially created," wrote Warsaw resident 15-year-old Miriam
Wattenberg in her journal in November 1940. "Where is the Moses who can free us from this
oppression?" Historians, archive footage and unearthed diary extracts found in metal boxes bring the
terrible era and conditions to life in this poignant documentary. _Ali Catterall_

24 HOURS IN A&E

9pm, Channel 4
A 23-year-old woman is rushed to Queen's Medical Centre after falling off a horse, which then rolled
on top of her. Meanwhile, a five-year-old boy is brought into A&E after badly cutting his hand at
school, and doctors urgently need to determine if a man in his 20s is having a stroke. _Priya Elan_

HOUSE OF THE DRAGON

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC
"The patient hunter gets the kill," says suave antagonist Ormund Hightower (James Norton), as the
reign of Rhaenyra Targaryen (Emma D'Arcy) fractures and frays. As attacks from within and without
threaten to take the queen's throne from her, Daemon (Matt Smith) wants to quite literally fight
fire with a lot more fire. _Jack Seale_

SECRET LIVES OF GYPSY WIVES

10PM, CHANNEL 4

[A glamorously dressed woman stands outside a pastel-pink terraced house]
Behind the very 00s title, this new reality series is surprisingly wholesome and boasts a bona fide,
Gemma Collins-esque star in Traveller influencer Trewley Precious Temple. This week, Trewley unveils
a beauty collab, the perfect project for someone who, says her manager, "came out of the womb in
lashes". _Hannah J Davies_

SECRETS OF THE CELEBRITY SEX TAPES

12.05AM, CHANNEL 4
When Love & Hip Hop: Atlanta reality TV star Mimi Faust featured in a 2014 sex tape, it triggered a
media frenzy in the US (not least because of an unintentional bit of slapstick involving a shower
rail). The likable Faust reflects on the whole tawdry affair. _Graeme Virtue_
