Taylor Swift’s remarkably ugly wedding has come and gone, disrupting the city during an historic
heat wave and taking up what will surely become long-term real estate in all of our minds as more
details emerge. But surely a wedding of this cultural magnitude, with about 1,000 mostly famous
guests, couldn’t have gone off without some light scandal. One of the first dramas (though not
_the_ first; that honor goes to Blake Lively’s apparent lack of invite) concerns Swift’s
co-producer and co-writer Jack Antonoff. He notably attended the wedding without his wife, actress
Margaret Qualley. They’ve yet to confirm a separation themselves, but sources have told People
that their marriage is over. Isn’t it so apt for a Swift wedding to precipitate a big breakup?
Finally, an era I’m compelled by.

I HATE TO LEARN A MAN’S NAME, BUT IT APPEARS I HAVE NO CHOICE: WHO’S JACK ANTONOFF? 

He’s Taylor Swift’s creative partner and Lena Dunham’s ex-boyfriend and Margaret Qualley’s
husband. There is a worryingly dark vibration around him that remains unproven, but I’ve never
been wrong about a man before.

AND HE’S MARRIED TO MARGARET QUALLEY, YOU SAY? WHO IS NOT A CHARACTER FROM _CHARLIE AND THE
CHOCOLATE FACTORY_? 

Correct. She’s Andie MacDowell’s daughter and an actress in her own right, starring in _The
Substance_ and _Maid_. They have an 11-year age gap, and she’s too hot for Jack, which we should
say now just to have it on the record as early as possible.

WHAT’S THEIR STORY? 

Qualley and Antonoff met in 2021 at a rooftop party. They went public in 2022 and got married the
next year. They’re a relatively low-profile couple, except for when Swift went to their rehearsal
dinner at a small venue in New Jersey and was followed by so many Swifties that the police had to
come and disperse the crowd. (Madison Square Garden makes more sense now, doesn’t it!!!)

NOW, WHY’S LENA DUNHAM IN HERE?

Earlier this year, Dunham released her second memoir, _Famesick_, in which she recounted her
long-term relationship with Antonoff from 2012 to 2017. Although he’s first described in the book
as a faithful and generous partner, he slowly becomes increasingly frustrated with Dunham and her
chronic illness and seems to start emotionally straying from their coupling. Dunham is sparing when
it comes to details about her ex, though she does admit to cheating on him near the end of their
relationship and lightly castigates him for spending too much time with an unnamed teenage pop star
that he’s working with who seems too emotionally tied up in him for it to be appropriate.

LORDE?

Lorde.

LISTEN, I SAW THE POWERPOINT.

We _all_ saw the PowerPoint, the one that very convincingly argued that Antonoff cheated with Lorde
while working on one of her records. Dunham even acknowledged the PowerPoint in _Famesick_. The
PowerPoint is now basically _The Da Vinci Code_ for women age 28 to 41.

WHAT DOES ANY OF THIS HAVE TO DO WITH SWIFT’S MEGA-MALL WEDDING? 

Antonoff and Dunham were there, at both the welcome dinner the night before the wedding and the
ensuing ceremony and reception at MSG. Qualley was notably not there, but some suggested that this
was because she was in Arkansas filming her new movie _King Snake_. That said, there were
unconfirmed Qualley sightings in upstate New York in the days before the wedding, suggesting that
she was perfectly available to be a plus-one.

IT’S TIME: GIVE ME THE SMOKING GUN.

Since Swift’s wedding, Qualley has quietly deleted photos of her wedding from Instagram, and
she’s been seen with actor Drew Starkey, her co-star in the forthcoming _King Snake_. They went
swimming.

WOW, SO MANY WHITE PEOPLE. WHO WOULD YOU SAY IS THE WHITEST? 

It’s a race to the bottom.

IS IT REALLY SUCH A BIG DEAL TO NOT GO TO A WEDDING WITH YOUR PARTNER? 

Usually, no, not necessarily. But it _is_ a big deal to skip the wedding of a famous pop star and
New York socialite of sorts, full of literally 1,000 of your peers and friends in the middle of a
city you live in, where your husband’s famous ex-girlfriend will be there months after publicly
reciting his many failures.

SO IF MARGARET QUALLEY AND JACK ANTONOFF GET DIVORCED, WHO KEEPS TAYLOR SWIFT IN THE SPLIT?

Antonoff. He will make some unavoidable music about it, something all the guys you know will use to
soundtrack their Instagram carousels after their first divorces. Qualley, in turn, would get a
compelling personal divorce narrative.

ALL RIGHT, SO WHAT’S LORDE UP TO? 

Touring in Spain right now, but if she were smart, she’d bring Qualley onstage to do her aerobics
routine from _The Substance_ while her spine detaches from her body. We need more girls’ girls out
here.
