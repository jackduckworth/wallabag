

====================================================================================================

Shania Twain's honest playlist: 'If Dancing Queen comes on, you rush out'

====================================================================================================

THE SONG THAT CHANGED MY LIFE
There's many critical songs on The Mission soundtrack, but Gabriel's Oboe [by Ennio Morricone] hit
me hard. I had to pull off the road, I cried like a baby. I didn't realise how powerful music could
be until that moment. I was 22.

THE FIRST SINGLE I BOUGHT
At Seventeen by Janis Ian. I reference it in my album Little Miss Twain and it says so much about
the emotions of a teenage misfit. I felt like her, I related – but I was also thinking: "Wow, I
have to some day write this well." It gave me a goal; I was so in awe. I made very little money,
babysitting, taking care of my neighbour's horse, singing in bars. I would get like 20 bucks, that I
would mostly give to my family, but I got that record for myself.

THE BEST SONG TO PLAY AT A PARTY
If Dancing Queen by Abba comes on and you're in the bathroom, you rush to get out, you can't miss a
second of it.

THE SONG I SECRETLY LIKE, BUT TELL EVERYBODY I HATE
Nobody ever wants to listen to The Phantom of the Opera, but I nerd out and listen to the whole
album. I have to do it by myself – it's sort of a secret.

THE SONG THAT GETS ME UP IN THE MORNING
When it comes to getting out of bed, pop always brings me somewhere new, like Harry Styles's Sign of
the Times. I love it.

THE SONG THAT MAKES ME CRY
Michael Jackson's Ben used to make me cry as a kid.

THE SONG I DO AT KARAOKE
I was always the kid singing in bars, the last thing I wanted to do was get up and pretend. I was
really purist about it. But I realised I'm missing out on all the fun. My favourite is Killing Me
Softly by Roberta Flack.

THE SONG I WANT PLAYED AT MY FUNERAL
I've been through a lot of grief in my life and there are intimate moments where you need to bawl
your eyes out. As the dead person, I want to say: this is a moment you cannot control, life goes to
shit sometimes. So I would choose Don't Worry, Be Happy by Bobby McFerrin; some things you can't
change.
_Shania Twain's Little Miss Twain is out now._
