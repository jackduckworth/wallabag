TV tonight: Michael Gove finally gets to play prime minister in The Wargame

====================================================================================================

THE WARGAME

9PM, SKY ONE
Using political figures from our recent past, this docudrama maps out a British response to an
escalating pattern of Russian aggression. Alongside Michael Gove (who gets to cosplay being prime
minister at last), politicos including Harriet Harman and Nicola Sturgeon take their places round
the cabinet table. Credible commitment to the concept is essential to make the drama elements work,
and all manage it reasonably well – particularly the unnamed Potus with Russian sympathies …
_Phil Harrison_

CLICKBAIT CLINIC WITH STACEY DOOLEY

8.30PM, BBC ONE
Leg-lengthening surgery. If you can stomach it, hardy Stacey Dooley invites us to watch the process
of a procedure (breaking both legs, inserting screws) to kick off this new series. She's looking
into various health and wellness trends going viral on social media – this one being
"heightmaxxing". _Hollie Richardson_

FINDING BRITAIN'S SLAVE TRADERS WITH DAVID OLUSOGA

9PM, BBC TWO
If anyone doubted the violence and moral corruption underpinning Britain's imperial past, this
two-part documentary will set them straight. David Olusoga investigates the register of British
slave traders, which illuminates the links between the history of modern institutions (including the
royal family) and the trade in human misery. Bleak but important. _PH_

999: WHAT HAPPENED NEXT

9PM, CHANNEL 4

[999: What Happened Next on Channel 4/.]
There are some brutal "it could happen to you" stories in tonight's episode, which traces the
aftermath of emergency calls. A woman getting ready for a Christmas night out is stabbed in her own
home and a gang stages a series of burglaries in an affluent area. _Hannah Verdier_

COLIN FROM ACCOUNTS

10PM, BBC TWO
Gordon (Patrick Brammall) and Ashley (Harriet Dyer) have split up from their very attractive
partners – but that doesn't mean they're ready to get back together. In fact, Ashley brings a date
to a sex party, which Gordon ends up at too. It unravels into another hilarious night of
excruciating events. _HR_

LUCY LETBY: SCAPEGOAT?

10PM, CHANNEL 5
After being found guilty of killing babies in her care, the UK's most notorious neonatal nurse was
jailed for life in 2023. But in the three years since Letby's sentencing, the prosecution's clinical
and statistical evidence has been repeatedly challenged. This one-off documentary re-examines key
points in a highly emotive case. _Graeme Virtue_
