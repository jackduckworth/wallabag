TV tonight: a gripping psychological thriller that keeps you guessing | Television | The Guardian

====================================================================================================

HUNTING ALICE BELL

9PM, CHANNEL 4 
The more unsavoury secrets that emerge about each member of the Not Alice Bell group, the harder it
is for each of them to avoid suspicion. When Graham Hunter (Ralf Little) escalates his campaign
against them, cracks start to appear. Ahead of Thursday's finale, this mystery is doing a credible
job of keeping us guessing. _Phil Harrison_

THE FUTURE WITH HANNAH FRY

7.30PM, BBC TWO 
Researchers estimate that the next five years will see 1 billion extra people online – but how
will they gain access to the internet? Prof Fry concludes her series by visiting a Google research
lab, where they're tinkering with transmitting information using light beams, and the streets of
Nairobi, where cheap smartphones and some ingenious data-sharing are enabling more and more kids to
get connected. _Jack Seale_

CELEBRITY RACE ACROSS THE WORLD

8PM, BBC ONE 
There's nowhere to go but up, as the celebrities celebrate reaching their fourth checkpoint with a
helicopter ride over the Great Barrier Reef. Then it's time to get to their next destination:
Toowoomba, Queensland. Sam and Kaz are in the lead with Junior and Princess – but which pair will
be split when one half manages to miss the boat south? _Hollie Richardson_

PORTRAIT ARTIST OF THE YEAR 2026

8PM, SKY ARTS 
Nine more hopeful artists have a brush with fame as they strive to capture the essence of three
celebrity sitters. Arbiter of taste Grace Dent, peppy prankster Munya Chawawa and candid comic Grace
Campbell are the models – but which one is "elegance personified" and who takes time out for a
pilates break? _Graeme Virtue_

MURDER CASE: FIRE IN THE SUBURBS

9PM, BBC TWO 
In the concluding episode, Cameron's girlfriend, Bex, wakes up from her medically induced coma to
hear the devastating news about her boyfriend. After the police change the status of the Hogmanay
house fire from "domestic fire" to "deliberate arson attack", will the identity of the masked figure
seen near the house finally be revealed? _Priya Elan_

FIRST DATES

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
You'd think that psychic Barbara would have seen a plumber coming into her life. But Jon is totally
unexpected when he walks into the First Dates restaurant tonight. Is that because he isn't the one?
On another table, sparks fly between expat Zac and drag queen Sab. _HR_

FILM CHOICE

WAY OF THE WARRIOR KID (MCG, 2026), APPLE TV 

[A young boy hangs from a ceiling bar watched by a fit-looking man dressed all in black]
There are distinct echoes of The Karate Kid in McG's light moral drama, based on a children's book
by a former Navy Seal. Marc (Jude Hill) is being bullied at school in San Diego and has zero
confidence, despite the best efforts of widowed mom Sarah (Linda Cardellini). Luckily, his uncle
Jake (Chris Pratt) is coming to stay … and he's in the special forces. Jake sets up a training
programme for his nephew to boost his self-worth – and teach him a few moves – but the officer
has his own demons to face. _SW
