TV tonight: Strictly's Amy Dowden and Carlos Gu join Celebrity Hunted

====================================================================================================

CELEBRITY HUNTED

9PM, CHANNEL 4

They've gathered a likable bunch for the seventh series of this survival show. Strictly's Amy Dowden
and Carlos Gu bring the glam, while comedian Brian Conley and and his daughter, Lucy, have an
advantage: "Only old people know who I am," he says. The other runaways are Love Island's Chris
Taylor and Toby Aromolaran, TV presenters JJ Chalmers and Sophie Morgan, property experts Scarlette
and Stuart Douglas, and comics Jen Brister and Laura Smyth._ Hollie Richardson_

BRITAIN'S STRICTEST SCHOOLS – PANORAMA

8PM, BBC ONE

Detention for looking out of a window? Last month, Devon and Cornwall's Athena Learning Trust
announced it will dissolve and transfer its schools to other organisations after parents complained
about strict disciplinary measures. Richard Bilton investigates the multi-academy trust and speaks
to former teachers and students. _HR_

EVOLUTION

9PM, BBC TWO

[A smiling man holding huge skull of a whale in front of darkening sky and sea]
How did we become intelligent? Which was the first animal to "think"? Chris Packham finds out in
another mind-blowing evolution lesson by focusing on the super smart dolphin. He rewinds billions of
years to light sensitive cells that developed into the first eyes, which would lead to the first
brains. Surprisingly, moths get a mention in this quest of cleverness. _HR_

LONG LOST FAMILY: BORN WITHOUT TRACE

9PM, ITV1

The family reunion show's spin-off series, focused on foundlings, returns. Nicky Campbell and Davina
McCall help David, who was abandoned in a London block of flats in 1960, trace his origins to France
and the Netherlands. For the week's other participant, Sarah, the story starts in a Leicester
telephone box in 1983. _Jack Seale_

SALLY MAGNUSSON REMEMBERS … MASTERMIND

9PM, BBC FOUR

For many, Mastermind will always be synonymous with the urbane Magnus Magnusson. This enjoyable
documentary takes a trip down memory lane with his daughter Sally, who recalls the show's origins,
her youthful excitement at its success and how it felt to have strangers yell "I've started, so I'll
finish!" at her in the street. _Phil Harrison_

SECRET LIVES OF GYPSY WIVES

10PM, CHANNEL 4
In the latest instalment of this documentary reality show, we learn a bit more about the
marginalisation Trewley faced at school. As she heads to Liverpool to see about potentially
launching a lipliner range, will her impostor syndrome get the better of her? _Priya Elan_
