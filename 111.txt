TV tonight: inside the disturbing plot to murder Holly Willoughby

====================================================================================================

THE PLOT TO KIDNAP HOLLY WILLOUGHBY

9PM, CHANNEL 4 
This deeply disturbing film enters the world of stalking and abduction chatgroups to explore the
2023 plot to kidnap, rape and murder Holly Willoughby. Fortunately, Gavin Plumb did his planning
with a man he knew as "David Nelson" – who turned out to be an undercover American cop. The film
explores the foiled plot and also examines the opportunities the authorities missed to arrest Plumb
for prior offences. _Phil Harrison_

DISPATCHES: LAWLESS BRITAIN? UNDERCOVER ON THE STREETS

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
The UK's official crime figures (which show a consistent fall in violent crime) and the often
hysterical news agenda tell strikingly different stories when it comes to safety on Britain's
streets. Ellie Flynn helms an investigation that looks at the causes of this disconnect and goes
undercover to sample the mood on the ground. _PH_

LUCY WORSLEY'S KEYS TO THE CASTLE

9PM, BBC TWO 
A familiar but still welcome combination of erudition and enthusiasm in this new series where Lucy
Worsley offers guided tours of four notable European castles. We begin with the Doge's Palace in
Venice – a place with a 24-carat gold leaf staircase for the fortunate, and a terrifying torture
chamber for troublemakers. _PH_

THE GILDED AGE

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC 
Social climber Bertha Russell may have conquered New York, but victory comes at a price, as Julian
Fellowes's lavish period drama continues. While her family deal with the fallout, Agnes the aristo
spots an opportunity to reclaim lost ground, thoroughly modern Marian strikes out in a new
direction, and Peggy attempts to win over her future in-laws. _Ali Catterall_

PEELERS: THE PSNI FOR REAL

10PM, BBC TWO 
Has any TV show done more for the image of the Police Service of Northern Ireland than the gripping,
moving drama that is Blue Lights? This series about the force certainly hopes to give it a run for
its money; this episode focuses on trouble outside the pubs and clubs of Portadown, as well as
resolving a potential street robbery. _Alexi Duggins_

LOVE CHEATS

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
The series exploring romantic scams returns for a third series and starts with a man pretending to
be the heir to Barbour and a EuroMillions winner. He wooed two women – kickboxer Wendy and
recruitment consultant Christine – who would later learn that he was, in fact, a homeless conman.
_Hollie Richardson_

FILM CHOICE

GIVE ME THE BALL! (LIZ GARBUS AND ELIZABETH WOLFF, 2026), DISNEY+

[Billie Jean King at full stretch for a forehand while playing in a darkened arena]
Unarguably the most important female tennis player in history, Billie Jean King gets a profile that
does full justice to her range of achievements. A 39-time grand slam champion with a phenomenal
can-do attitude, she led the fight for equal prize money with the men and birthed the entire women's
professional game. King is also a wonderfully eloquent and candid interviewee, so directors Liz
Garbus and Elizabeth Wolff just set the camera down and let her tell all. Binge eating, sexuality,
Elton John – just a few of the topics touched on in a remarkable life story. _Simon Wardell_
