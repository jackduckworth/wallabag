TV tonight: survivors from the Bondi beach terror attack share their stories

====================================================================================================

TERROR ON BONDI BEACH

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
This harrowing documentary recalls the events of 14 December 2025 when terrorists attacked a Hanukah
celebration taking place in Sydney. It's also a celebration of the courage of the first responders
– and of the many people who put their own lives on the line to save those under attack. The
survivor testimony and the attack footage make it a difficult watch but it's a striking one, too.
_Phil Harrison_

ALDI: THE NEXT BIG SELLER

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
Anita Rani, Jimmy Doherty and Aldi's chief commercial officer, Julie Ashfield, cast their eye over
another slew of indie producers hoping to hit the big time by being stocked in stores nationwide.
Angling for space in the Healthy & Wholesome section are flavoured sauerkraut, a cookie-dough
dessert with a Malaysian twist, and the alluring mystery that is a "vegan egg"_. Jack Seale_

SHETLAND

9PM, BBC ONE 
GP Raymond Linden disappeared nine years ago. Now his remains have turned up in a car boot. Ashley
Jensen's DI Calder and Tosh (Alison O'Donnell) investigate. Could Ray's best friend, Dr Graeme
Gould, be a suspect? After all: "He got over it pretty quickly, jumping into bed with his wife first
chance he got." Then Tosh goes missing … _Ali Catterall_

RISE OF THE ROCKSTAR CHEF

9PM, BBC TWO 
We've been promised the beefiest reunion ever broadcast in this highly entertaining look at the
sensational culinary rise of Marco Pierre White, Gordon Ramsay and Heston Blumenthal. But when these
three do sit down in the final episode, there's a lot of love in the room (in between the occasional
head-butting). _Hollie Richardson_

THE GOOD DAUGHTER

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC

[A woman wearing black sitting next to an older man reading a book, in front of a brick wall]
The brilliant Rose Byrne (Sam) and Meghann Fahy (Charlie) continue their quest as believable sisters
thrown into unbelievably horrible situations. This week, there's a glimpse into their past in New
York, complete with fractious moments. Meanwhile, the school shooting investigation continues with
Sam looking into Kelly's family background. _Hannah Verdier_

CUNK ON CINEMA

10PM, BBC TWO 
Philomena Cunk's definitive laugh-a-minute history of cinema picks up from the second world war and
film propaganda: "Daffy Duck heroically killed Hitler, thereby stopping the war in its tracks." She
then wades into the new world of Hollywood stars and auteurs, with the help of Benedict Cumberbatch
and Michael Bay ("These are odd questions," he says). _HR_
