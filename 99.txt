No really, he said that:

Senator Mark Kelly seemed to suggest it would have been better if those sailors hadn’t wanted to

questioning that it was an “insult” to call it “mitigations”:

> “When you say it’s mitigation, you’re compartmentalizing something that is clearly a mental
> health failure,” she said. “The fact that a service member, a sailor, went overboard is beyond
> a mitigation.”

Cao whined that one of those sailors wasn’t even trying to kill himself. “What was he trying to
do?” asked Gillibrand. “Swim to Guam?”

This is the military under Secretary Shitfaced Hegseth and Donald Trump, and also Hung Cao.
