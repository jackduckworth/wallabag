TV tonight: the two women who made a podcast about their ex-partner's lies

====================================================================================================

10PM, CHANNEL 4
Amber Rasmussen thought Brandon Johnson was "the full package" when they met on Tinder. They quickly
got engaged but there was one problem: his "crazy ex" Athena Klingerman was apparently making wild
demands. As this three-part true-crime series shows, it was – shocker! – Brandon causing all
sorts of trouble. The women later teamed up to make a podcast exposing his lies. _Hollie Richardson_

RENOVATION RESCUE

8PM, CHANNEL 4
Having previously been part of the Stacey Solomon Cinematic Universe, the home-fix show is now
presented by Vogue Williams (who was on I'm a Celebrity, but has degrees in construction management
and quantity surveying) and Luke Mabbott (who was on Love Island, but is a trained heating
engineer). First, they're in Northamptonshire. _Jack Seale_

TALKING PICTURES: SEVENTIES LEGENDS PART II

8PM, BBC FOUR
Head-spinning terror in The Exorcist! Mohawk-menacing Robert De Niro in Taxi Driver! A first space
voyage with Star Wars! The 70s was an epic decade for cinema and Celia Imrie has a great time
getting stuck into the moments that made it. She also remembers a particularly memorable awards
speech from Jack Nicholson. _HR_

WHO DO YOU THINK YOU ARE?

9PM, BBC ONE

[Harriet Walter in Who Do You Think You Are?]
Actor Harriet Walter didn't know much about her mother's history – but she makes extraordinary
discoveries about her Italian family heritage. The second world war is her starting point, where
Walter's mother played a crucial secret part in the D-day landings, and it compels her to remember
her in a brand new light. _HR_

THE FIVE-STAR WEEKEND

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC
A double bill of the starry summer drama, with grieving chef Hollis (Jennifer Garner) taking the
girls for dinner, followed by a local dive bar. The secrets in the group have started spilling, but
is mysterious Gigi (Gemma Chan) going to reveal all about the affair? Or will Brooke (D'Arcy Carden)
do it for her? _HR_

THE HUNTING PARTY

9PM, U&ALIBI
A disturbing drama that will have you checking under the bed. It's been two months since serial
killers escaped a secret prison – and former FBI profiler Bex is on the verge of exposing the
cover-up. Meanwhile, "boogeyman" killer Ron Simms (Will & Grace's Eric McCormack) is looking for
victims. _HR_
