THE MADAME BLANC MYSTERIES

9.05PM, CHANNEL 5 
Series five of the cosy crime drama. Somebody has murdered the mayor of Sainte-Victoire and dumped
her body in the village fountain, which has put a dampener on antique-dealing sleuth Jean's (Sally
Lindsay) Christmas Eve wedding plans. Still, she joins the investigation, and though a local builder
is under suspicion, she believes there may be a different motive. _Ali Catterall_

BREAST CANCER SCANDAL: EXPOSING THE HARM

7pm, BBC Two 
BBC health correspondent Sharon Barbour presents a disturbing one-off documentary about women in
County Durham whose mastectomies were found to have been unnecessary. "I feel as if I've been
butchered," says one woman, while what happened is called a "horrific outlier" by a former
consultant surgeon. _Hannah J Davies_

THE MARTIN LEWIS MONEY SHOW LIVE – BUDGET SPECIAL

7.15PM, ITV1 
Given the likelihood that any modern budget will be a mild tinkering at the edges of the UK economy,
the challenge for home-finance guru Martin Lewis might be finding enough to talk about – but where
viewers have been given choices to make, there's nobody they trust more to help make them. _Jack
Seale_

TASKMASTER

[Nina Conti smiles while holding a sealed envelope at a wooden table]
9.05PM, CHANNEL 4 
Why is Nina Conti insulting a piece of fruit? Will Chloe Petts ever be able to light a match? And
when did Richard Ayoade get so good at drawing shoes? It can only be more weird goings-on in
Taskmaster, which ends next week with one of the stars winning the golden Greg Davies trophy.
_Hollie Richardson_

IT GETS WORSE

10.05PM, CHANNEL 4 
This week's double bill of Leo Reich's raucous coming-of-age London sitcom starts as it should: with
a warehouse sex party. How will mates Sam, Abi and Ethan, each with their own expectations, fare?
Then, a video from the night's proceedings comes to light at Sam's open-mic night. _HR_

A NIGHT IN WITH SALLY LINDSAY AND SURANNE JONES

10.05PM, CHANNEL 5 
Channel 5 is experimenting with video podcasts (expect more of them), including this new series in
which Sally Lindsay has a nostalgic chat with stars about their greatest telly moments. Her first
guest is Suranne Jones, who will hopefully dish some dirt on the upcoming return of Doctor Foster.
_HR_
