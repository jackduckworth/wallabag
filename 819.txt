TV tonight: Jill Scott, Big Zuu and Sam Ryder take on Taskmaster

====================================================================================================

TASKMASTER'S NEW YEAR TREAT

9pm, Channel 4
TV chef Big Zuu, former Lioness Jill Scott, Eurovision star Sam Ryder, dictionary corner's Susie
Dent and Strictly winner Rose Ayling-Ellis are the plucky contestants out to win a golden Greg
Davies bust in this two-part special – from prodding a dangerous animal to playing the
glockenspiel. Plus, which one of them will sweat over a foot pump for 20 minutes? _Hollie
Richardson_

WOULD I LIE TO YOU?

7.30pm, BBC One
Messrs Brydon, Mitchell and Mack are joined by Yinka Bokinni, Harriet Kemsley, Julie Hesmondhalgh
and Chris McCausland. Whether you're wondering if McCausland has ever saved a Mo Salah penalty or
pondering Bokinni's dental arrangements, it's an effortlessly enjoyable half-hour. _Phil Harrison_

2025: THE YEAR FROM SPACE

7.30pm, Channel 4
TV, aerial: it's the annual look at the world from above via striking satellite images. Alongside
the bleak extent of human displacement in Gaza and the vast scale of California's wildfires is the
Hindu festival of Kumbh Mela, which attracted 600 million people to the Ganges. _Hannah J Davies_

TOM READ WILSON'S MAGNIFICENT JOURNEY

8pm, Channel 5
Hugely likable I'm a Celebrity runner-up Tom Read Wilson sets off on the grand tour of Europe, a
rite-of-passage journey taken by aristocrats in the 17th to 19th centuries. He starts his tour in
Paris, where he learns the basics of etiquette from a countess._ HR_

JANE MCDONALD: FROM POLE TO POLE

9pm, Channel 5
TV's beloved Jane McDonald is ringing in the new year with a new adventure: over six episodes,
she'll be travelling from Antarctica to the Arctic, beginning with a helicopter flight over
snow-capped mountains and icebergs, plus a relaxing glass of champagne on board her Discovery yacht.
There's also time for a song, of course. _Ellen E Jones_

AMANDA & ALAN'S GREEK JOB

[Amanda Holden and Alan Carr stroking a donkey at a sanctuary in Corfu]
9.35pm, BBC One
"Another dump, another poo on the floor, another sense of doom." Amanda Holden and Alan Carr's third
renovation is also their biggest job. They're in Corfu, with a crumbling property they want to turn
into something fit for a Mamma Mia! film. But first they're going to have to gut the entire house.
_HR_
