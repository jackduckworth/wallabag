TV tonight: Fightland is 50 Cent's big and bloody boxing drama

====================================================================================================

FIGHTLAND

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC
"Boxing is a criminal enterprise," says Nicholas Pinnock's slick crime kingpin Kingsley Marshall.
"Always has been, always will be." It's a highly debatable claim but typical of this big, bloody and
somewhat basic drama executive-produced by Curtis "50 Cent" Jackson, which is set at the nexus of
pugilism and gangsterism. It stars Howard Charles as "Duke Kilroy", a champion boxer in search of
revenge after the murder of his brother. About as subtle as a punch in the face. _Phil Harrison_

DRAGONS' DEN

8PM, BBC ONE
Another day in the Den and more Dragons to impress. This week includes a nervous husband-and-wife
team with their worryingly expensive water-saving device, a health food entrepreneur who hasn't made
that much profit but has already had a grant from Sainbury's, and a street art impresario who has
gone viral. _Priya Elan_

THE BIG DEAL WITH STEPH MCGOVERN

8PM, BBC TWO
The business journalist's surprisingly brutal art dealership competition lands in sunny Margate this
week, where the five remaining pairs need to sell limited-edition prints to the public. Gavin gets
off to a strong start: "I don't even know what a print is." _Hollie Richardson_

A HOUSE THROUGH TIME

9PM, BBC TWO

[A man in a blue raincoat standing by a pier with the sea behind him]
Events take a dark turn as the 20th century arrives at the home in Calton Hill, Edinburgh. David
Olusoga shows how the residence manifests the economics of the era – it's now multiple-occupancy,
which means overcrowding and trouble. There's also an insight into the dangers of working in a
prison. _PH_

LUDWIG

9PM, BBC ONE
It's murder on the dancefloor, as a dreamy wedding takes a nightmarish turn. Leave it to
master-puzzler John (David Mitchell) who is now working as a crime scene consultant. But the fact
that every wedding guest saw the victim creates a problem: "That's too many variables!" _HR_

HIT POINT

9PM, U& DAVE
Howard Overman's unusual crime thriller is heating up nicely, as compromised cop Leo continues to
pretend to investigate crimes in which he is involved. The consequent bittersweetness of the
sparking chemistry between Leo and his partner Bella is cannily written. _Jack Seale_
