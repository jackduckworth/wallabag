TV tonight: a stirring documentary about two mothers taking on Big Pharma

====================================================================================================

MUMS V BIG PHARMA

9PM, BBC TWO 
A still unresolved story of institutional failure and relentless campaigning, this documentary
explores the damage done to generations of children by sodium valproate, a drug that was licensed
for prescription to people with epilepsy despite concerns having arisen after testing. At the heart
of the story are Janet Williams and Emma Murphy, two mothers who refused to be deterred by official
inertia (and, at times, outright scorn) and whose robust lack of self-pity makes this film stirring
rather than dispiriting. _Phil Harrison_

FIRE AND FLOODS: OUR EXTREME WEATHER

8PM, BBC ONE 
It still doesn't feel as if the media has got to grips with the gravity of humanity's environmental
plight. This documentary is at least trying, however, by exploring 2026's extreme weather, which has
encompassed everything from intense downpours to our parched and fiery summer. Is it merely a taste
of what's to come? _PH_

JAMIE'S 20 MINUTE MEALS

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
More one-take Jamie recipes as the chef combines virtuosity with accessibility to impressive effect.
The showpiece this time is a garlic, rosemary and chilli-chicken pasta dish: it looks good in its
own right, but Jamie makes time for a little flair in the shape of a vermouth spritz on the side.
_PH_

PIERRE

9PM, CHANNEL 4

[A woman of colour in a green patterned dress and a man of colour in a dark suit, pink shirt and
brown tie talking to each other]
In the finale of David Harewood's sound, jazz-infused legal drama, duty solicitor Pierre's search
for the truth around the death of his young client Michael leads him to someone closer to home –
which prompts an uncomfortable confrontation. But with everything else that's going on in his
personal life, will he get a final answer? _Hollie Richardson_

THE GILDED AGE

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC 
The addictive period hokum continues, with what will be the start of a long process for Larry (Harry
Richardson) and Marian Brook (Louisa Jacobson). They've had sex! Without being married! This is a
scandal up with which the posh folk of 1880s New York will not put. Can the lovers cling to their
places in society? _Jack Seale_

PETE WICKS: FOR DOGS' SAKE

9PM, U&W 
A disturbing catch-up with the 11 shih-tzus who were rescued from a home last year. The dogs were
withdrawn, terrified and possibly interbred. The Towie alumnus also gets back in touch with Spud,
the spunky staffy crossbreed we met last autumn. Can they train him out of his bad behaviour? _Priya
Elan_
