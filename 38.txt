TV tonight: how three rockstar chefs sparked a foodie revolution

====================================================================================================

RISE OF THE ROCKSTAR CHEF

9PM, BBC TWO 
"Gastronomy is the French Foreign Legion," says Marco Pierre White. "They don't question you too
much." In the 1980s, the culinary desert of the UK saw the rise of three difficult, brilliant chefs
(Pierre White was joined by Gordon Ramsay and Heston Blumenthal) who triggered a foodie revolution.
This three-parter doesn't quite avoid presentational, era-specific cliches, but it does capture the
energy generated by the trio's rise. _Phil Harrison_

ALDI: THE NEXT BIG SELLER

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
This new series offers a glimpse into the inner workings of a supermarket – and if that kind of
thing is your bag of pick'n'mix, it's fascinating. We begin with sweet treats: from mochi to
waffles, how does a new product reach the shelves? Anita Rani and Jimmy Doherty are on the panel
sitting in judgment. _PH_

DEAR HENRY: OUR FIGHT FOR JUSTICE – A TONIGHT SPECIAL

9PM, ITV1 
The awful 2025 murder of Henry Nowak became a political football after being exploited by the far
right. But this touching programme rightly repositions Nowak's death as what it primarily is: a
terrible family tragedy. Paul Brand talks to Nowak's family as they share their memories of a
much-missed son and brother. _PH_

TASKMASTER

9PM, CHANNEL 4

[A man in a yellow jacket popping out from behind a door]
Series 22 hasn't been TM's strongest outing, with Richard Ayoade's commitment to being contrary
often wearing thin. But there is much to enjoy in this final episode (including a final visit to
London's Museum of Water and Steam), before the winner receives that coveted trophy replica of Greg
Davies's bonce. _Hannah J Davies_

BIG BROTHER: LIVE FINAL

9PM, ITV2 
Another series of the all-seeing reality series comes to an end, having been dogged with numerous
complaints about the contestant Omar's problematic behaviour (Women's Aid said Big Brother risked
"presenting abuse as a form of entertainment"). For anyone still watching (or hatewatching,
perhaps?), a reunion episode also airs on Friday night. _HJD_

IT GETS WORSE

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
The double-bill season finale of this brilliantly insufferable sitcom begins with the cosmically
self-involved Ethan's insistence on wearing a tank top to the funeral. OK, but it _is _black, guys?
That's the tipping point, before Abi, Sam and Ethan end up having the mother of all barnies, just
before entering the church._ PE_
