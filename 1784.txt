In fact, Trump endorsees losing was a running theme of last night.

somebody named Eric Barlow. NBC News notes that this is a bit of a pattern now in governor’s
races, as previously “Trump’s preferred candidates also lost in Iowa, Georgia and Minnesota, and

was headed for defeat.”
