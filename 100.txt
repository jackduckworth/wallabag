Yes, the reporting said that Mary Peltola’s campaign had engaged in an effort to find everybody
named Dan Sullivan in all of Alaska and convince them to run for the Senate, because her opponent
the incumbent Republican is Dan Sullivan. This could prove very confusing for Alaska voters at the
ballot box, as they are gaping morons, at least in the eyes of the Republican Party.

“Do we want our Senator Dan Sullivan?” asks an Alaskan at the polls. “Or do we want Dan
Sullivan the salmon-fishing drag queen with a heart of gold?” “Oh, we might like the drag queen
with the heart of gold who smells of fish!” This was an illegal conversation.

Throughout the process, Donald Trump, the king of fairness, asserted that the only way for such a
crime against democracy to be rectified would be to give all the votes for anybody named Dan
Sullivan, and also the votes for any other candidate _not_ named Dan Sullivan, to Senator Dan
Sullivan. His orgasmically panting pomeranian Todd Blanche at the Department of Justice tried with
all his might to sue Alaska out of doing democracy so hard it couldn’t fight back.

As if the votes had not already been cast, as if the Peltola campaign had actually done something
illegal — fuck you, _New York Times_ — as if it would have even mattered in the end.

It did not. Mary Peltola reached 50 percent last night, an ancient mathematical number defined as
“more than all Dan Sullivans combined” and she fucking won the fucking thing, fucking
officially. She will be the 56th Democratic senator in the new Congress, only the ninth Alaskan
senator in the history of the whole state, and the first Alaska Native. Along with Peggy Flanagan in
Minnesota, Peltola will be one of two Native women in this Senate.

The AP called it. CNN called it. VoteHub noted that it had called the race four days ago, because
geez.

But sure, let’s make sure she wins by almost five points before we say anything, in case any
dumbass Alaskan voters were so confused by the proliferation of Dans Sullivan that they had a panic
attack in the voting booth and somehow accidentally started casting thousands of illegal votes for
Peltola.

If you need to see a mathematical explanation for how Mary Peltola illegally reaching 50 percent of
the vote means that she beat all the Dan Sullivans, including the fish-smelling drag queen Dan
Sullivans, and also all the guys named Gerald, please consult the internet, or calculators.

In the interest of transparency, we’ll even paste the numbers right here, so you can see what kind
of trickery the Peltola campaign got up to, by winning outright:

> In preliminary results Tuesday night with 290,003 ballots counted, Peltola received 50.1% of
> first-choice votes, or 144,302 votes — more than all other candidates combined. Sen. Sullivan
> trailed with 45.8% of the vote (131,844 votes); Republican perennial candidate Gerald Heikes
> received 2.1% (6,105 votes); and retired Petersburg teacher Dan J. Sullivan received 1.7% (4,760
> votes).

Sick, just sick.

As we said, this makes 56 Democratic senators for the Democrats, or four more senators than the
minimum required to make John Fetterman the most irrelevant fucking loser in America.

“We did it!” she said, and then detailed the manner and enthusiasm with which she disemboweled

Together, we're going to put an end to the era of self-serving politicians lining their own pockets
