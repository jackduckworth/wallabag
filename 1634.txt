TV tonight: an eye-popping account of Katie Price in the Playboy mansion

====================================================================================================

KATIE PRICE: NOTHING TO HIDE

9PM, SKY DOCUMENTARIES
Part two of Katie Price's rollercoaster life story. After an eye-popping account of her time in the
Playboy mansion, Price recalls finding out she was going to become a single mum and the public
judgment that came with it. Gareth Gates also sits down to speak about their fling – "I was on
cloud nine!" – and what happened when Price later sold their story to the press. Then we reach the
I'm a Celebrity stint, where Price met a pop star called Peter Andre. _Hollie Richardson_

SILENT WAR: THE SHADOW OF ATOMIC BOMBS

10.40AM, PBS AMERICA
With volatile politicians in power across the world, nuclear paranoia is back. This timely two-part
documentary is a shadow history of nuclear weaponry, exploring the chaos that has resulted from bomb
tests. In places, it's clear that the effects have almost constituted a detonation by stealth. _Phil
Harrison_

AMBULANCE: CODE RED

8PM, CHANNEL 5
You know things are serious when Thames Valley Air Ambulance critical-care teams are mobilised. This
headlong series wraps up itslatest run with unflappable paramedics dealing with the aftermath of a
horrific bonfire accident before realigning a mangled ankle. _Graeme Virtue_

FIRE COUNTRY

9PM, SKY WITNESS

[Three people in yellow fireproof jackets and yellow hardhats in front of a fire engine]
It's season four of the US drama focused on rugged hosing in the company of Cal Fire, the
organisation responsible for ensuring California is ablaze slightly less often. This week, the
Station 42 team respond to a zipline accident. That may not sound like a very combustible situation,
but throw in some illegal fireworks and you've got yourself a wildfire. _Jack Seale_

MY FLOATING HOME

9PM, MORE4
This week on the relaxing property show, Mark Evans goes to Scarborough to meet a retired engineer,
Tony, who wants to restore a 40-year-old narrowboat to live a quiet life on the water with his
partner, Emily. But first, a bit of chaos, including the complication that the boat is moored 130
miles away from where they live. _HR_

MYSTERIES UNEARTHED WITH DANNY TREJO

10PM, SKY HISTORY
More slightly bizarre fun in the show where the Heat and Machete actor Danny Trejo introduces people
recalling odd mysteries. This time, the cases involve fossilised vomit, a sewer blob and a toy
discovered inside a man's lung. _HR_
