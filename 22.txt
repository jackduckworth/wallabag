TV tonight: who will win Celebrity Traitors? It's time to find out

====================================================================================================

THE CELEBRITY TRAITORS FINALE

8PM, BBC ONE 
And just like that, the second Celebrity Traitors has reached its finale! It might not have had Alan
Carr, but Miranda Hart warmed hearts, Ross Kemp upped the paranoia and James Acaster became a sex
symbol. There's one more round table, then the finalists learn their fates in the flames. _Hollie
Richardson_

LANDWARD

7.30PM, BBC TWO 
Dougie Vipond visits Aberdeenshire to meet Mike Duxbury. After losing his sight at six years old, he
became one of the first blind students to be accepted into agricultural college. After hundreds of
job rejections, he and his partner, Ness, founded the Inclusive Farm, for people with disabilities
and diverse needs. _Priya Elan_

GARDENERS' WORLD

8PM, BBC TWO 
It's the penultimate episode before Monty Don waves a green-fingered goodbye. In advance of that,
Adam Frost is making finishing touches to his new garden before the growing season ends, while
Rachel de Thame celebrates the chrysanthemum. _HR_

THE WALKING DEAD: DEAD CITY

9PM, SKY ONE 
After last week's alternative reality detour, it's back to blood-spattered business as usual in
zombified New York. Bourbon-voiced brigand Negan (Jeffrey Dean Morgan) is holed up in a dive bar
after his latest rage-induced murder. Can Maggie (Lauren Cohan) convince him to surrender before a
trigger-happy posse comes a-knockin'? _Graeme Virtue_

WAR

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC 
More of the glossy divorce drama that arguably prioritises style over substance. Carla (Sienna
Miller) goes public about her split from Morgan (Dominic West) with an outburst on live TV that gets
her trending online. Or, as she puts it: "This is a talkshow, so let me bloody talk!" _Hannah J
Davies_

THE GRAHAM NORTON SHOW

10.40PM, BBC ONE 
It's a "bunny boiler" reunion in the studio this week, as Michael Douglas takes a seat beside Glenn
Close on the red sofa. They're joined by Kieran Culkin (with whom Close stars in the new Hunger
Games film) and Naomi Ackie. Music comes from Sugababes. _HR_

FILM CHOICE

THE END OF OAK STREET (DAVID ROBERT MITCHELL, 2026), 9.10AM, 8PM, SKY CINEMA PREMIERE

[Anne Hathaway, holding a metal rod, leads three teenagers walking outdoors near houses]
It's 1982 and the Platt family – dad (Ewan McGregor), mum (Anne Hathaway) and two anxious kids –
are existing in a state of suburban stasis, until the stakes are raised by a bunch of dinosaurs
stomping through their neighbourhood. How and why these dinos have suddenly appeared are questions
for a different movie. This one – made by Mitchell, director of 2014's cult horror hit It Follows
– is mostly concerned with big, silly blockbuster fun, Spielberg references and the resilience of
an unhappy family forced to band together. _Ellen E Jones 
_

APOCALYPSE Z: NEW WORLD (CARLES TORRENS, 2026), Prime Video 
The Spanish-language zombie flick Apocalypse Z: The Beginning of the End was a huge international
hit for Prime Video in 2024. In this sequel, also directed by Torrens, much of the population has
taken refuge in the Canary Islands, after the outbreak of the blood-borne TSJ virus. Roberto (Carlos
Alcaide) joins a special return mission to the mainland, which soon becomes a scramble for survival
as the infected hordes march on. Perhaps returning fan favourite Lúculo the cat (still played by
Pedrito the cat) can come to the rescue? _EEJ_
