Anybody who has ever cared for a loved one with dementia knows about Post-Its and labels. Notes to
remind them which is the bathroom, which is the kitchen, notes to remind them why they are where
they are, where familiar people are. It’s often the questions they’re asking over and over
again, the questions their loved ones are happy to answer, but might go a little bit apeshit if they
have to answer them 140 times per day.

It’s literally on the Post-Its website, how they can be helpful to people with Alzheimer’s and
their caregivers.
