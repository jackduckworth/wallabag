Well, That Was One Way for Ivanka Trump and Her Niece to Spend the Weekend

====================================================================================================

This Labor Day weekend, not one but two Trump decendents managed to earn the internet’s ire, in
suspiciously similar ways. Donald Trump’s daughter Ivanka Trump, presumably taking a short break
from her project of transforming Albania into a luxury tourist destination, posted a video to social
media of her daughter riding a horse on a gorgeous beach, which she made that much more cinematic by
soundtracking it to a Coldplay song (“Viva La Vida,” in case you were wondering). Elsewhere, her
brother Donald Jr.’s eldest daughter, Kai Trump, shared a vlog that featured a tour of her new
digs, a very un-dorm-like luxury apartment where she’s hanging her hat (and golf clubs) as a
freshman at the University of Miami.

In both cases, the backlash arrived swiftly, with people online and critics complaining that the
Trump family lives lavishly while the rest of the country can barely afford the necessities. These
objections are nothing Ivanka and Kai haven’t heard before—in fact, they’re utterly
predictable at this point. So you have to wonder why they persist in posting things they can expect
to lead to another round of outrage. In other words: Have the Trump children and grandchildren
discovered rage-bait?
