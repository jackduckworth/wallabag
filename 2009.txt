TV tonight: how did 9/11 affect the experience of being Muslim in Britain?

====================================================================================================

BEING MUSLIM AFTER 9/11

9PM, BBC TWO 
"There was a time, 25 years ago, when being a British Muslim was OK," says journalist Mobeen Azhar.
"We were on the cover of magazines, getting elected to parliament and even captained the England
cricket team." Then 9/11 happened. He and his film-making partner, Masood Khan, have made this
documentary to explore how the terror attacks affected the Muslim community – including their own
experiences – and why they still reverberate today. _Hollie Richardson_

PANORAMA: ROMAN ABRAMOVICH – HOW HE HID HIS FORTUNE

8PM, BBC ONE 
New evidence from a data leak sees Panorama sift through hundreds of documents that may have been
falsified to conceal the vast wealth of the ex-Chelsea owner and oligarch. Will it manage to set the
news agenda like the accusations levelled against Man City's owners did? Abramovich disputes the
allegations and denies any wrongdoing. _Alexi Duggins_

JAMIE'S 20 MINUTE MEALS

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
The chef continues his real-time cookery class with a steak night dinner, complete with a shallot
and brandy sauce and a serving of garlicky gnocchi chips. Then he puts together a crispy trout with
green veggies and a tomato and tarragon salsa. Both are ready in 20 minutes. _HR_

CLICKBAIT CLINIC WITH STACEY DOOLEY

8.30PM, BBC ONE 
Is there truth behind influencers' claims that testosterone levels are at a historic low? T-maxxing
is the next social media "wellness trend" investigated by Dooley. She meets men who believe that
artificially increasing their levels will boost bone density, sex drive and muscle mass, before
putting questions to the experts. _HR_

PIERRE

9PM, CHANNEL 4

[A young man films a police officer and a man, on his phone, from behind a crate]
"I just don't want to be the one to tell the aunt that her nephew wasn't the boy she thought he
was." Charismatic duty solicitor Pierre (David Harewood) looks into the murder of his teen client
Michael – and questions if he was wrong to believe he wanted to turn away from crime. It only
makes him want to dig deeper for answers … _HR_

MICHAEL PALIN IN THE PHILIPPINES

9PM, CHANNEL 5 
Palin spends his third and final week on the southern island of Mindanao: a no-travel zone and the
home of disgraced former president Rodrigo Duterte. In the city of Davao, the Monty Python star
meets the DJ behind Budots, the perky song and twerking-like dance craze, improbably inspired by
Duterte's death squads who hunted for drug dealers. _Priya Elan_

FILM

KILLING ANNA (SAM BENSTEAD, 2026), 11.05PM, CHANNEL 4
"I weaponise myself, my trauma." Annsar Shahhoud, a Syria-born researcher in genocide studies at the
University of Amsterdam, is the focus of this remarkable documentary. After being sent videos of
massacres by the Assad regime, she set up a fake Facebook account (under the name of Anna Sh),
befriended Syrian officials and militia, and got them to admit their role in the mass murders of
civilians in Damascus. The toll on Shahhoud is clear, but so is her will to bring the truth to
light. _Simon Wardell_
