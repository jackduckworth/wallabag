But let’s look at some numbers. As this fact sheet from Fidelity explains, if you’re married and
you want to take the No Tax On Tips deduction, you have to file jointly. If Sharon Simmons saved
$3-4,000 in (federal) taxes on that $11K, then she and her husband would necessarily have to be in

wouldn’t be subject to federal income taxes in the first place. Note also that No Tax On Tips
doesn’t apply to payroll taxes or anything else besides federal.
