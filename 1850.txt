TV tonight: Joe Lycett broadcasts live from an East End pub – what could possibly go wrong?

====================================================================================================

LIVE FROM THE LYCETT ARMS

10PM, CHANNEL 4 
In a bid to celebrate and save pubs, Joe Lycett is opening an East End boozer. The goings-on inside
the Lycett Arms will be broadcast live for an hour every Friday, as the new landlord welcomes
celebrity managers and guests, as well as familiar punters. This could go anywhere – Lycett failed
to complete the exam needed to obtain an alcohol licence and issued a plea for help on Instagram.
Let the chaos begin! _Hollie Richardson_

MARIN ALSOP'S PICK OF THE PROMS

7PM, BBC FOUR
The American maestro – who, infamously, was namechecked in Cate Blanchett's Tár – talks about
being the first female conductor to lead the Last Night of the Proms, in 2013, and discusses her
favourite moment of this season: pianist Yunchan Lim's opening-night performance of Ravel's Piano
Concerto. _Priya Elan_

YOU'RE KILLING ME

8PM, U&DRAMA
Another fun murder mystery to solve with boomer Allie (Brooke Shields) and gen Z Andi (Amalia
Williamson). When a car dealer is found dead in his garden, the crime-solving duo recognise
similarities to a murder from Allie's first novel. Will it lead them to the killer? _HR_

THE MARLOW MURDER CLUB

9PM, BBC TWO
The two-part mystery that began last week concludes. And things are as hair-raising as they ever get
in this cosy crime context: there's clearly going to be another murder unless the team can prevent
it, so Tanika takes a gamble and recruits a group of civilian advisers. Will they be too late? _Phil
Harrison_

THE RAINMAKER

[Two women in a courtroom setting wearing smart suits]
9PM, ITV1 
Not just a doppelganger for OG Rainmaker Matt Damon, British actor Milo Callaghan also ably leads
this highly watchable US legal drama. This week sees Rudy (Callaghan) discover some unlikely
evidence for the wrongful death suit, before a courtroom showdown opposite ex-boss Leo (Mad Men's
John Slattery). _Hannah J Davies_

GRIMSBURG

12.15AM, SKY ONE
When useless nepo baby Otis is forced into the, well, force after pressure from the mayor, a
horrified Flute (Jon Hamm) concocts a series of ever more extreme schemes to "crush a rich kid's
dreams". Meanwhile, a cold-ridden Harmony takes too much of Marvin's "special cough syrup" and
conjures up her son's imaginary skeleton friend, Mr Flesh (Alan Tudyk). _Ali Catterall_

FILM CHOICE

HARVEST (ATHINA RACHEL TSANGARI, 2024), 11.05PM, BBC TWO

Athina Rachel Tsangari's adaptation of Jim Crace's novel is shot like a homage to 1970s British folk
horror films – complete with Wicker Man-style animal masks and rituals. But the horror turns out
to be more economic and social than supernatural. In an unnamed rural Scottish village, the
diffident Master Kent (Harry Melling) rules benignly over the agricultural workers, including his
former servant, the reserved Walter (Caleb Landry Jones). But then Kent's cousin (Frank Dillane)
arrives with plans to enclose the common land and raise sheep. The inequities of power, and the way
the dispossessed can blame outsiders for their lot, come to the fore in a brooding, earthy tragedy.
_Simon Wardell_
