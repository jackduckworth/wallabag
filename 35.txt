TV tonight: the final episode of gloriously daft sketch show Ellie & Natasia

====================================================================================================

ELLIE & NATASIA

10PM, BBC TWO

This excellent sketch show refuses to outstay its welcome: the third episode is also the last. But
as a consequence, its hit rate is high, and when it's good, it's hilarious. The pair are gloriously
daft but retain a keen eye for absurd modern manners: check their po-faced, all-female reimagining
of Only Fools and Horses ("the repetition of the word 'plonker' actually felt quite problematic to
us")._Phil Harrison_

THE FUTURE WITH HANNAH FRY

7.30PM, BBC TWO

The mathematician investigates how technology is helping scientists decode animal communication,
from African elephants to sea lions and a "talking" dog in California. As Fry relates, an
"incredibly powerful" bit of AI could soon turn us all into Doctor Dolittles. "And this technology
is coming …" _Ali Catterall_

CELEBRITY RACE ACROSS THE WORLD

8PM, BBC ONE

[Two men wearing T-shirts on a beach with wooden chalets and trees behind them]
After a short delay waiting for Peter Andre's children to catch up, the vaguely famous travellers
embark on the second leg of the journey across Borneo. Cane it down the main road or choose an
apparently more circuitous route? With most of the contestants apparently more interested in seeing
exotic primates than getting from A to B quickly, it doesn't much matter._Jack Seale_

PORTRAIT ARTIST OF THE YEAR 2026

8PM, SKY ARTS

Another group of celebrities arrive for a sitting as this competition gets into its stride. This
time, host Stephen Mangan invites Rylan Clark, Alison Balsom and Jamie Lloyd into the studio. It's a
cheerful and interesting affair, balancing irreverence and just about enough artistic seriousness.
_PH_

MURDER IN BELFAST: SISTERS SEEKING JUSTICE

9PM, BBC TWO

The second part of a family's search for justice after the IRA-related murder of Robert McCartney
outside a Belfast pub in 2005. "The public are very fickle and you'll be forgotten about next week,"
his sister Gemma recalls Gerry Adams allegedly saying. But as names of the killers came to light,
the story would make its way to the White House. _Hollie Richardson_

ROB & ROMESH VS BOWLING USA

9PM, SKY ONE

You've probably fallen in love with "Robesh" all over again after The Celebrity Traitors. In this
episode, they investigate bowling – which, as Rom observes, is basically the "US darts". Pros
treat it with reverence, as evidenced when the bantering duo meet "the pro with the fro", Kyle Troup
(he's white). _Priya Elan_
