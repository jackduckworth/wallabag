TV tonight: Linda Cardellini stars in a Friday the 13th prequel from A24

====================================================================================================

CRYSTAL LAKE

9PM, SKY ATLANTIC 
A Friday the 13th prequel series produced by A24? Halloween has come early for gorefest fans. Linda
Cardellini plays small-town single mother Pam Voorhees, who takes her son Jason (Callum Vinson) to
summer camp where she works as a cook. But one day Jason drowns in the lake. Fast forward a year,
and Pam is grieving but clings to a shred of hope as her son's body still hasn't been found. When
two cops knock on her door to tell her they have discovered Jason's remains, things take a huge turn
– and it turns out there's more to her and Jason's story than meets the eye … It has all the
jump-scare beats of a classic 70s slasher movie and Cardellini gets stuck in – blood, guts and
all. _Hollie Richardson_

JAMIE'S 20 MINUTE MEALS

8PM, CHANNEL 4 
A cheesy quiche with smoky ham and a punch of mustard, and chicken breasts flambéed in a brandy and
onion sauce are the two main courses knocked up by chef Jamie Oliver in real time in this week's
episode. And to wash it all down: a prosecco and pomegranate spritz. _HR_

CLICKBAIT CLINIC WITH STACEY DOOLEY

8.30PM, BBC ONE 
Meet Marcello, a biohacker who is part of the methylene blue movement – he believes the supplement
that's the colour of Sonic the Hedgehog can boost brain function. So much so, he gets it infused
into his body in a car park in Surrey. Evidence for its health benefits is scant and you will be
passing bright green pee for days. Dooley investigates. _Priya Elan_

ABERFAN: THE VILLAGE THAT FOUGHT BACK

9PM, BBC TWO

[Head and shoulders shot of a woman wearing glasses and a blue denim top]
As the 60th anniversary of the horrifying Welsh disaster is marked, it's customary to remember
Aberfan as simply a tragedy. This deeply affecting documentary recasts it as a scandal, too: an
inevitable result of long-running industrial negligence. The memories of eyewitnesses are
interspersed with the story of the tireless campaign for accountability._ Phil Harrison_

PIERRE

9PM, CHANNEL 4 
Poor Pierre (David Harewood): his wife and boss are losing patience with the duty solicitor as he
attempts to get to the bottom of young client Michael's death – all while ignoring his recent
diabetes diagnosis. But could some footage on Michael's phone provide a breakthrough? A solid legal
drama, lifted by a suave Harewood._ HR_

WALTER PRESENTS: SOVIET JEANS

12.05AM, CHANNEL 4 
The first episode of this Latvian drama gets a terrestrial airing to mark its launch on Channel 4's
streaming service. It's the classic love story: hidden-market trade guy falls in love with theatre
director gal … and so does a KGB agent. Expect plenty of drama from this tale of Riga in the 70s.
_Alexi Duggins_
